Choosing between the 6-bit DAC and the PB1 square wave for Ladybug.

Hardware reference (selector switch, DAC paths, PB1) is in release/reference/coco3/sound.

Choosing for Ladybug

The original arcade Lady Bug has melody snippets (during attract, between stages, when collecting heart, on death) and many short SFX (eat dot, eat vegetable, enemy moves through gate, death). A first pass:

  • PB1 square wave for short SFX — cheap, fast, doesn't need a sample table.
  • 6-bit DAC driven from the Vbord IRQ for one melody voice (a stepped-sine table). One channel of melody is faithful to the arcade's mono audio output.

Decision deferred to coding-architect. Both share the same IRQ context, which is the constraint that matters: anything we do in sound has to fit in the 60 Hz Vbord handler's slack budget — see timing.

Selector setup gotcha

From a cold reset, BASIC sets up usable defaults for the audio selector; we will not rely on those — we'll initialise both PIAs explicitly.

Sources: coco-asm-tepolt ch. 10 (DAC, selector, comparator, listings 10-2 SLTON); coco3-asm-tepolt ch. 7 (split FF22).
