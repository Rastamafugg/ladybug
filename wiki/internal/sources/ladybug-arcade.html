MAME background capture — 2026-07-17

The locally installed MAME 0.220 ladybug driver and ROM were sampled by scripts/capture_mame_maze.py. The helper reads background code RAM $D000-$D3FF and colour RAM $D400-$D7FF once a complete attract-mode maze remains stable for at least ten frames. Frames 2545–2558 produced the accepted raw 2,048-byte capture with SHA-256 d8129bb9f57ce51b0355101115af7eab538b854d31771f6f51917fa072f686f3.

MAME's raw visible rectangle is tile columns 1–30 and rows 4–27. Applying ROT270 produces the arcade 24×30 visible grid. Maze frame rows are 2–27; the CoCo 192×192 adaptation retains rows 3–26 and represents the omitted horizontal border rows at the top and bottom pixel edges. Raw and derived data are in assets/arcade/maze_capture.bin and assets/arcade/maze_capture.json.

This capture is authoritative for background tile placement and colour attributes. It does not contain sprite RAM, collision rules, or a semantic distinction between walls, dots, gates, and decorative tiles.

Phase 4 identified the player independently by recording a live MAME gameplay frame and pixel-matching every decoded sprite, attribute, and flip combination. This selected sprite 0 with attribute 0 as the canonical player/life form; sprite 3 is a rotated directional form. The driver maps $7000-$73FF as write-only sprite RAM, so a normal CPU-space dump cannot read the video device's private copy.

StrategyWiki gameplay ingest — 2026-07-18

StrategyWiki describes four enemies beginning in the den. A clockwise White-to-Green border circuit releases them one at a time, with circuit speed increases on stages 2 and 5. Enemies wander until the player is close, then pursue; they cannot rotate turnstiles. It also claims that enemies inside the den cannot kill a player who enters it. These AI, timing, and den-collision details require MAME verification.

Each stage contains six synchronously coloured bonus circles: three letters and three hearts. The stated cycle is Red for less than one second, Yellow for about four seconds, then Blue for about seven seconds. Red matching letters advance SPECIAL, Yellow matching letters advance EXTRA, and Blue hearts advance ×2, ×3, then ×5. Off-colour collection still awards 800/300/100 points according to Red/Yellow/Blue but does not advance a target. Flowers award 10 times the current multiplier; vegetables are not multiplied.

The page says normal stage completion requires all flowers and all six circles, while completing either word immediately advances the stage. EXTRA grants a life and resets. It describes SPECIAL as a free game; Ladybug's locked CoCo v1 adaptation remains 10,000 points because a cabinet-credit reward is not meaningful in the standalone port.

Skulls are stationary and lethal to either side. The page claims two on stage 1, increasing gradually to six after stage 10, with each added skull replacing food. A skull-killed enemy returns to the den and is released again; a skull-caused player death removes remaining skulls. After all four enemies leave, the vegetable appears. It freezes but does not disarm enemies, may recur after a killed enemy cycles through the den, and is followed by an enemy speed increase when its tune ends. These progression and recurrence rules are now explicit MAME capture targets.

The vegetable table confirms eighteen stages from cucumber 1,000 through horseradish 9,500 in 500-point increments. The page also documents separate one- and two-player start buttons; alternating two-player support remains an explicit CoCo v1 scope decision.

GameFAQs rules ingest — 2026-07-18

The FAQ states that an arcade board has 121 food positions before replacements. Stage load chooses three hearts, three letters, and two through six poison skulls at randomized food positions; every other position contains a flower. The CoCo crop has 117 corresponding placement candidates because the adapted 24×24 maze omits four arcade food positions. Its stage-one result is therefore 109 visible flowers after six bonuses and two skulls replace eight candidates.

Letter selection is one random member of X/T/R, one of S/P/C/I/L, and one of E/A. The skull table is two on stage 1, three on stages 2–4, four on stages 5–8, five on stages 9–17, and six on stage 18 onward.

Contact with poison kills the player and removes every remaining skull; collected flowers, bonuses, gates, score, and stage state remain. The FAQ also reports that gameplay briefly pauses to register a bonus pickup. This is what the earlier “MAME-unverified pickup pause” note meant: it is a secondary-source claim, not an implemented or confirmed delay. The installed MAME ROM remains authoritative: measured holds are exactly 30/150/420 frames for Red/Yellow/Blue, while a controlled pickup capture is still required before adding any pause.

A controlled stage-one skull collision captured on 2026-07-18 identified the exact death art and timing. Arcade sprite 57 occupies frames 879–908; sprites 58–63 follow for five frames each through frame 938; White wing sprites 64–68 begin on frame 939 and fly upward. The compact evidence is stored in assets/arcade/death_animation_reference.json.

MAME gameplay calibration — 2026-07-18

scripts/capture_mame_gameplay.py drives MAME 0.220 through logical input fields and captures every rendered frame plus background RAM. MAME's ioport_field::set_value accepts logical pressed/released values regardless of the driver's active-low wiring; passing electrical mask values prevents Start and directions from registering. The reusable plans are under scripts/mame_plans/.

The accepted stage-one baseline records one pixel of displacement on every 60 Hz frame. A right request at frame 620 is retained until the first legal centre at frame 655 and changes direction without a stopped frame. Gate plan 17 approaches the left end of a horizontal bar from the south; background codes rotate at frames 729–730 and the player continues north from maze row 22 through the new west passage to row 18. The same background delta proves that complete gate graphics use five cells per orientation: horizontal character 52 extends to offset (-2,0), while vertical character 59 extends to offset (0,-2). Their combined dirty-redraw footprint is seven cells. The completed vertical state uses characters 59,61,50,64,62,255,63 across that union, proving that character 50 is contextual maze background rather than a blank cell. scripts/verify_arcade_fidelity.py checks these facts against gameplay_reference.json, turn_reference.json, and gate_reference.json.

Installed program-ROM inspection identified the intermediate gate tables at Z80 addresses $0CD5 and $0CDE. Each is a row-major 3×3 slash or backslash frame; zero entries retain the existing background. MAME frame 729 contains the intermediate frame and frame 730 contains the completed perpendicular frame, establishing a one-frame duration. Arcade characters 64–74 are the diagonal artwork visible as the final eleven entries in the rotated sheet's third column.

Driver evidence: MAME 0.220 Lady Bug driver; sprite/background evidence: MAME 0.220 Lady Bug video device.
