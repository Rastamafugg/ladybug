2026-10-02 — Six-bit page values

Partial source ingest: local tcc1014.c, SHA-256 c8433d3ffb186e6dc3cb2d5df306dcdca5b0b7af3a492172d0e17f8a6c1961b4, lines799–804. MMU writes store *CPUD & 0x3f; reads return (*CPUD & ~0x3f) | mmu_bank[A & 15]. The upper two read bits come from the bus and are not part of the six-bit page identifier. Compare raw & 0x3f to a page number; retain the raw byte separately.

BUG-086 E5 observed raw task0 PARs B8,AC,AD,AE,AF,B4,BE,BF while read_gime_state reported38,2C,2D,2E,2F,34,3E,3F. Masking the raw bytes agrees at all eight slots. The helper-entry rejection from an exact byte comparison was an observer error. This source finding does not explain the earlier worklist timeout or prove succession behavior.
