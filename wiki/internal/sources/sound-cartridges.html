External Sound Cartridges internal

Sources consulted 2026-07-26 for low-CPU Ladybug audio.

Sources and extracted facts

  • Tandy Speech/Sound Cartridge owner's manual. The cartridge accepts commands at $FF7E, reset at $FF7D, contains eight sound buffers of up to 64 bytes, and can execute stored sound-data or PSG-register strings. Its AY-3-8913 provides three tone channels, noise, and a hardware envelope.
  • Tandy Speech/Sound service manual and CoCo 3 modification note. Most units need a voltage-inverter and clock-input modification at 1.78 MHz; without clock correction, sound effects run one octave high.
  • DragonPlus SuperSprite FM+ product specification. The board contains YM2149 PSG and YM2413 FM chips, supports $FF5x MPI mode and $FF7x native mode, and mixes audio into its routed A/V output.
  • Tandy Multi-Pak owner's manual. Software register $FF7F independently routes SCS and CTS/CART among four slots. This permits a RAM-resident game to retain or disconnect its ROM slot while routing SCS I/O to a sound board.
  • Orchestra-90 hardware map. Its left/right outputs are write-only 8-bit DACs at $FF7A-$FF7B. This requires audio-rate CPU writes and is not a synthesis offload.
  • Local XRoar 1.10 source: src/gmc.c, src/mpi.c, src/sn76489.c, and src/ay891x.c. GMC already contains an emulated 4 MHz SN76489; even/odd SCS writes select banks/program sound. MPI independently routes ROM and I/O. AY891x synthesis exists, but no Speech/Sound or SuperSprite cartridge profile and no YM2413 core are present.
  • MAME CoCo Speech/Sound device. This BSD-licensed reference models the TMS7040, its firmware, 2 K RAM, AY-3-8913, SP0256, sound-activity circuit, and host interface at $FF7D-$FF7E.
  • MAME SuperSprite FM+ device. This BSD-licensed reference establishes MPI-mode addresses: YM2413 at $FF56-$FF57, V9958 at $FF58-$FF5B, YM2149 address/data at $FF5C-$FF5D, and video select at $FF5E.

Limits

Physical coexistence with the Ladybug GMC image has not been tested. Product availability can change. MAME confirms the emulated register map, but A/V routing and timing must still be verified against the current user guide and a physical CoCo 3/MPI setup before locking a backend ABI.
