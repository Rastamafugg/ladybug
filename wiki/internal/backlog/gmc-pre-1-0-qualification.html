GMC qualification before 1.0 Deferred

Review remaining GMC emulator and hardware qualification before the official 1.0 release.

Current evidence

The user reports the game appears to work during interactive play in XRoar. The current complete-profile ROM is 65,536 bytes with SHA-256 3794d6feac156514f1dcf97ca0f83f5b0dfeba6561cfb93be38a586f2938d161. It passes the recorded build, capacity, static, audio, and focused startup checks.

The retained MAME 0.220 paired marker pass is for the historical highscore-test candidate ROM with SHA-256 cba9070ee43b9d05f4697e097394691b38f41a74966bea778e5cc55b147270d7. The current complete-profile ROM has not been verified in MAME. The full automated complete-profile XRoar verifier also retains a 10-second timeout before start-preempts-ranking-load; this does not identify a cause. XRoar's parity-based SCS handling cannot distinguish $FF40 from $FF50, and physical GMC-compatible hardware has not been tested. See BUG-113 and BUG-115.

Items for pre-1.0 review

  1. Decide whether to qualify the current complete-profile ROM in MAME. If approved, name its expected startup marker and deadline in the assignment before running the emulator; retain the ROM hash, command, and marker evidence. The previous HST marker result is not a complete-profile MAME result.
  2. Decide whether to diagnose the automated start-pre-emption timeout in BUG-115. Its proposal remains unapproved; the timeout alone does not establish a gameplay defect.
  3. Decide whether direct physical GMC-compatible hardware qualification is required for 1.0, because XRoar cannot discriminate the two even-address aliases.

These are deferred review candidates, not newly approved implementation scope or established release blockers. BUG-113 and BUG-115 remain Deferred until the pre-1.0 review assigns any required work.
