Approval brief

Objective and motivation

The unattended loop must show an intact part-one level-start screen, enter a normal-engine demo, terminate through normal death, and return to attract.

Reproduction

  1. Launch without input.
  2. Allow attract and instructions to complete.
  3. Observe the demo level-start screen and transition into demo gameplay.

Observed versus expected

Observed: the level-start screen appears, then becomes corrupted and the game crashes. This is one user observation; the exact crash PC, stack, MMU/PAR state, and framebuffer owner are not yet captured.

Expected: the level-start frame remains intact until its deadline, gameplay initialization publishes a valid level one, deterministic input drives normal player and enemy services, death occurs by enemy or skull, and attract resumes.

Scope

In: level-start-to-demo transition, gameplay initialization reuse, framebuffer and PAR ownership, presentation actor underlay state, deterministic route input, death handoff, and bounded crash capture. Out: new demo-only movement/collision/rendering, live gameplay rule changes, and instruction choreography.

Discriminating test

Capture state before level-start publication, immediately before and after each demo initialization service, at first demo tick, and at crash. Record PC, S, DP, PAR5, active/back/pending owners, presentation mode/context, and a framebuffer hash. Reproduce naturally; forced death-only probes do not satisfy this bug.

Acceptance criteria

  • The natural level-start frame remains pixel-stable until its configured transition.
  • The demo enters normal level-one gameplay without memory, stack, PAR, or framebuffer corruption.
  • A deterministic route advances through ordinary player, enemy, collision, render, and death services.
  • Natural enemy or skull death returns to the attract title; both causes also pass forced verification.
  • Credit and start pre-emption remain atomic throughout level-start and demo.

Assignment contract

Approved objective; unassigned pending a bounded natural crash capture and a complete Ready contract. Expected artifacts are src/presentation_runtime.s, relevant resident gameplay source, scripts/verify_gmc_boot.py or a focused natural demo probe, this ticket, index, and log.

Verification and completion

Pending natural crash trace, corrected natural loop recording, both forced death paths, clean capacity checks, and regression results.
