Approval brief

Objective and motivation

State the user-visible or project outcome and why it is needed now.

Evidence and baseline

Record observed facts, source revision, artifacts, units, and whether values are maxima, averages, projections, or single observations. For persistent A/B rendering, label scenario phase and executed worklist first and buffer owner second; require a controlled crossover before attributing timing to owner identity.

Scope

In: exact behavior and artifacts. Out: explicit exclusions.

Scenarios and edge cases

List normal, boundary, rare, replay/integration, failure, and regression cases.

Options and recommendation

Compare viable options, risks, capacity effects, rejected alternatives, and the recommended approach.

Acceptance criteria

State measurable behavior, performance, diagnostic, capacity, compatibility, and evidence requirements.

Type-specific evidence

Bug: exact reproduction, observed result, expected result, environment/revision, hypotheses, and discriminating test.

Feature: user-visible behavior, compatibility/migration, defaults, failure behavior, and affected workflows.

Assignment contract

  • Approved objective and non-goals: repeat without modification.
  • Owned artifacts: exact files, modules, wiki pages, generated outputs, and worktree/branch.
  • Implementation constraints: architecture, capacity, compatibility, authority, and prohibited scope.
  • Required commands and scenarios: build, focused tests, integration tests, and forced rare cases.
  • Evidence: artifacts to retain and freshness metadata.
  • Commit: one bounded commit; push/merge authority stated explicitly.
  • Completion report: result versus target, tests, commit, capacity, rejected experiments, and residual risk.

Verification and completion

Record current-revision commands, results, integration checks, target verdict, evidence links, commit hashes, residual risk, and justified final status.

Decision log

  • YYYY-MM-DD — Draft created; source and rationale.
