Ticket Template internal

Copy this structure for features, bugs, performance, tooling, documentation, or research.

[TYPE-NNN] Short outcome

  • Status: Draft
  • Created / updated: YYYY-MM-DD / YYYY-MM-DD
  • Priority / order: P2 / unassigned
  • Roadmap or source: link
  • Owner: unassigned
  • Dependencies: none identified
  • Bug risk class: Compact, Standard, or High-risk, with the risks that determine verification scope; omit for non-bug tickets.

Approval brief

Objective and motivation

State the user-visible or project outcome and why it is needed now.

Evidence and baseline

Record observed facts, source revision, artifacts, units, and whether values are maxima, averages, projections, or single observations. For persistent A/B rendering, label scenario phase and executed worklist first and buffer owner second; require a controlled crossover before attributing timing to owner identity.

Scope

In: exact behavior and artifacts. Out: explicit exclusions.

Scenarios and edge cases

List normal, boundary, rare, replay/integration, failure, and regression cases. Map every bug scenario to one distinct risk; prefer pairwise coverage and justify every Cartesian interaction.

Options and recommendation

Compare viable options, risks, capacity effects, rejected alternatives, and the recommended approach.

Acceptance criteria

State measurable behavior, performance, diagnostic, capacity, compatibility, and evidence requirements.

Test cards — explicit user sign-off

Repeat a short card for each new/materially changed gating scenario. Apply approved DOC-014; see examples. Ticket approval may approve its cards together. Do not mark Ready until card approval and relevant baseline qualification are recorded.

  • Purpose / distinct risk / type: what defect or behavior this gate discriminates; unit, source/oracle, integration, performance, robustness or manual.
  • Setup: exact revision/profile/artifact and fixture identities, prerequisites and controlled inputs/RNG/chooser/clock/framebuffer history where relevant; disclosed forced state; bounded setup success marker. No random-route discovery requirement.
  • Expected result: independent oracle, actual observed marker/output, justified tolerance, pass/fail rule and deliberately broken negative control. Good/broken fixtures may qualify a new regression gate before the product fix.
  • Command / cost / stop: exact command, entire named-phase deadline including nested reads/waits, callback cap where needed, labeled duration measurement or estimate, retained last-state evidence and existing reassessment triggers.
  • User-owned manual session: record the artifact, procedure, expected observations and user-reported result. Apply DOC-014's manual-session rule; impose no session deadline, agent monitoring or duration report unless requested. Automated components retain their approved bounds and requirements; unobserved criteria remain pending.
  • Result / coverage: separately report product assertion failure, setup/observer failure, missing coverage, approved waiver and manual observation; missing required scenarios remain incomplete/failed, manual cases pending until user results. Exceptions allow listed failures without requiring them.
  • Approval / owner: explicit approval reference, automated/manual split, exact owned files, reused harness and dependency order. Material target/tolerance/state-write/setup authorization/coverage/cost-risk changes require renewed approval; ordinary mechanics within the approved contract do not.

Qualify only relevant existing gates against their baseline before reuse. Executors cannot invent gates or repair unrelated debt. Non-gating diagnostics remain labeled/bounded; after two rejected probes/two tool timeouts/material expansion, stop and reassess without retries until pass, hidden matrices or new route frameworks.

Type-specific evidence

Bug: exact reproduction, observed result, expected result, environment/revision, hypotheses, confirmed cause or explicit uncertainty, discriminating test, Compact/Standard/High-risk class, reclassification triggers, and smallest sufficient verification set. A Compact ticket may combine required fields but may not omit them.

Feature: user-visible behavior, compatibility/migration, defaults, failure behavior, and affected workflows.

Assignment contract

  • Approved objective and non-goals: repeat without modification.
  • Owned artifacts: exact files, modules, wiki pages, generated outputs, and worktree/branch.
  • Implementation constraints: architecture, capacity, compatibility, authority, and prohibited scope.
  • Required commands and scenarios: build, focused tests, integration tests, and only those forced rare cases mapped to a distinct risk.
  • Risk controls: cumulative probe count, verifier-size reassessment if any, and stop/reassessment conditions tied to evidence.
  • Evidence: artifacts to retain and freshness metadata.
  • Commit: one bounded commit; push/merge authority stated explicitly.
  • Completion report: result versus target, tests, commit, capacity, rejected experiments, and residual risk.

Verification and completion

Record current-revision commands, results, integration checks, target verdict, approved scope exceptions, concise evidence links or hashes, commit hashes, residual risk, and justified final status.
