Approval brief

Objective and motivation

State the user-visible or project outcome and why it is needed now.

Evidence and baseline

Record observed facts, source revision, artifacts, units, and whether values are maxima, averages, projections, or single observations. For bug work, distinguish repository evidence from user-reported token usage and label quota projections as estimates when telemetry is unavailable. For persistent A/B rendering, label scenario phase and executed worklist first and buffer owner second; require a controlled crossover before attributing timing to owner identity.

Scope

In: exact behavior and artifacts. Out: explicit exclusions.

Scenarios and edge cases

List normal, boundary, rare, replay/integration, failure, and regression cases. Map every bug scenario to one distinct risk; prefer pairwise coverage and justify every Cartesian interaction.

Options and recommendation

Compare viable options, risks, capacity effects, rejected alternatives, and the recommended approach.

Acceptance criteria

State measurable behavior, performance, diagnostic, capacity, compatibility, and evidence requirements.

Type-specific evidence

Bug: exact reproduction, observed result, expected result, environment/revision, hypotheses, confirmed cause or explicit uncertainty, discriminating test, Compact/Standard/High-risk class, projected weekly-token share, ceiling, reclassification triggers, and smallest sufficient verification set. A Compact ticket may combine required fields but may not omit them.

Feature: user-visible behavior, compatibility/migration, defaults, failure behavior, and affected workflows.

Assignment contract

  • Approved objective and non-goals: repeat without modification.
  • Owned artifacts: exact files, modules, wiki pages, generated outputs, and worktree/branch.
  • Implementation constraints: architecture, capacity, compatibility, authority, and prohibited scope.
  • Required commands and scenarios: build, focused tests, integration tests, and only those forced rare cases mapped to a distinct risk.
  • Cost controls: approved ceiling, cumulative probe count, verifier-size exception if any, and stop/reassessment conditions.
  • Evidence: artifacts to retain and freshness metadata.
  • Commit: one bounded commit; push/merge authority stated explicitly.
  • Completion report: result versus target, tests, commit, capacity, rejected experiments, and residual risk.

Verification and completion

Record current-revision commands, results, integration checks, target verdict, cost result versus ceiling, approved exceptions, concise evidence links or hashes, commit hashes, residual risk, and justified final status.
