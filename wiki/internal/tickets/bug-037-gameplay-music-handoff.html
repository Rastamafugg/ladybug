Required natural sequence and proposed arbitration

  1. Natural credit/start enters gameplay and services the level-start music on the same single-owner clock used during play. Effects cannot seize a musical channel or prevent a musical event from being admitted.
  2. On maze completion, latch completion once, keep the completed maze visible and suspend further gameplay simulation/events. Existing active music finishes. Pending unique musical events, including the completion cue, play to their authored end.
  3. Only after active and pending gameplay music is empty may next_stage reset state and publish the next-level screen. No maze redraw or state reset occurs underneath a held note. The transition occurs exactly once.
  4. For terminal death/game-over, retain the maze until death choreography and required music both finish. Explicit reset may abort and silence immediately. Ordinary input cannot skip the musical completion barrier.

Proposed policy for approval: active music is non-preemptible by other cues; waiting music is selected by existing cue priority, equal priority in event order. Repeated requests for the same active/pending cue are coalesced rather than restarting its note. Effects never displace music in admission or mixing. Reserve bounded admission for every distinct reachable musical event; prove the bound from existing callers. Nonmusical stop/system events must not accidentally replace an unfinished tune; preserve their intended final-silence/reset effect. This policy is a proposal, not a claim about current behavior.

Music is identified from the cue manifest class, not merely the exclusive flag: cue 16 is an exclusive system stop. Cue streams remain unchanged. Continuous or stale event polling must not keep the barrier alive indefinitely. Existing effects may continue to age while muted; expired effects must not burst after music ends.
