Required natural sequence and proposed arbitration

  1. Natural credit/start enters gameplay and services the level-start music on the same single-owner clock used during play. Effects cannot seize a musical channel or prevent a musical event from being admitted.
  2. On maze completion, latch completion once, keep the completed maze visible and suspend further gameplay simulation/events. Existing active music finishes. Pending unique musical events, including the completion cue, play to their authored end.
  3. Only after active and pending gameplay music is empty may next_stage reset state and publish the next-level screen. No maze redraw or state reset occurs underneath a held note. The transition occurs exactly once.
  4. For terminal death/game-over, retain the maze until death choreography and required music both finish. Explicit reset may abort and silence immediately. Ordinary input cannot skip the musical completion barrier.

Proposed policy for approval: active music is non-preemptible by other cues; waiting music is selected by existing cue priority, equal priority in event order. Repeated requests for the same active/pending cue are coalesced rather than restarting its note. Effects never displace music in admission or mixing. Reserve bounded admission for every distinct reachable musical event; prove the bound from existing callers. Nonmusical stop/system events must not accidentally replace an unfinished tune; preserve their intended final-silence/reset effect. This policy is a proposal, not a claim about current behavior.

Music is identified from the cue manifest class, not merely the exclusive flag: cue 16 is an exclusive system stop. Cue streams remain unchanged. Continuous or stale event polling must not keep the barrier alive indefinitely. Existing effects may continue to age while muted; expired effects must not burst after music ends.

Design direction, options and risks

Replace the unguarded normal_stage exit with a bounded music-drain handoff and an explicit completion query. Keep the PSG sequencer on exactly one established frame clock across the retained maze phase; do not merely add a second call that doubles note speed or depends on rendering time. Keep mappings and runtime installation valid before every call. Queue storage and query placement require the architectural checkpoint; no new module, broad scheduler rewrite, FIRQ audio migration or unrelated refactor is included.

Rejected direction: continuing the tune during next-level drawing fixes the hold but violates the requested finish-before-exit sequence. Silencing the note or truncating the cue also fails. A narrower completion-only patch is lower cost but would not satisfy the requested general music-priority contract.

Material risks: duplicate ticks, event loss, infinite drain, system-stop interference, shared low-RAM installation and scarce resident/presentation space. Current build guards include a 920-byte copied audio engine, 8192-byte audio page and presentation ending before $1E00; actual remaining space must be freshly measured. Existing memory-map prose contains historical allocations; source/build guards and current manifests establish active ownership. Any required module expansion, loader change or hard-limit relaxation returns this ticket to Proposed.

Measurable acceptance and minimal scenarios

  • Natural last-pickup → current tune completion → completion tune → next-level publication: zero next-stage state changes or publication before required music ends; exactly one handoff. Risk: sequencer freeze and early exit.
  • Every authored wait consumes exactly its encoded number of sequencer ticks, using the existing command-stream convention; no doubled/missing tick across gameplay and retained-maze phases. Compare PSG writes/slot progress with the unchanged streams and retained audio. Risk: held notes or altered speed.
  • Forced simultaneous effects and musical events, active music plus lower/equal/higher-priority music, duplicate event and full effect FIFO: no lost unique musical event, no active tune restart/truncation, correct waiting order. Use one bounded combined fixture plus a duplicate control. Risk: admission differs from channel priority.
  • Natural start cue, terminal death and next-level entry plus a reset during drain: no stale tone, deadlock or call into the wrong installed owner. Reset is the explicit abort case. Risk: cross-phase ownership and stop semantics.
  • Default complete/6809/keyboard build and current capacity guards pass. Report audio/resident/presentation sizes and remaining bytes before/after, and maximum audio-service cycles versus the pre-fix fixture. No phase exceeds its verified frame budget. A new timing regression requires a measured fix or renewed approval, never silent acceptance.

Natural input/progression evidence is mandatory; forced rare cases supplement it. Reuse scripts/verify_audio_runtime.py for stream/placement assertions and the existing GDB-MCP path for timing and handoff captures. Existing static verification alone is insufficient. Hardware listening is reported separately; emulator trace success is not a physical listening pass.
