Approval brief

Objective and motivation

The high-score/credit screen must appear only in response to a credit edge and must show one coin per available credit at the TMX-authored locations in the required order.

Reproduction

  1. Launch without input and observe whether the high-score screen or any coins appear.
  2. Press 5 or 6 once and inspect the first coin.
  3. Add credits repeatedly through the maximum, then press 1.

Observed versus expected

Observed: the high-score screen appears on load; the left half of the middle coin column appears at the right edge, and the full right column is absent. Current generation hard-codes nine destinations at column 33 and rows 2 through 18, while runtime advances by one constant destination delta. This cannot represent three TMX-authored columns or the required left-to-right, bottom-to-top order. The current TMX has one tile layer and no machine-readable coin object layer, so the generator is not validating its constants against authored slot metadata.

Expected: no high-score screen or coin appears before input. The first 5/6 edge opens the screen and shows exactly one coin. Each later edge adds the next coin left-to-right within a row, then bottom-to-top, stopping at the final authored location and maximum credit count. Player 1 start consumes one credit and leaves the attract flow for level start. Attract restarts only after a played game reaches game over and any qualifying high-score flow completes.

Scope

In: credit-screen entry and exit policy, credit saturation, machine-readable metadata at the existing valid coin locations in coco-high-score-screen.tmx, nonuniform destination records, coin draw order, clipping, and natural key-edge verification. Out: moving the visually authored coin slots, visible score/name records, name entry, two-player mode, and battery-backed credits.

Scenarios and edge cases

  • No input: no credit screen and no coins.
  • First 5 or 6 edge: one credit and one coin at the first authored slot.
  • Held key: no repeated credits; alternating and simultaneous credit keys follow the approved edge policy.
  • Multiple credits: one additional coin per accepted edge, ordered left-to-right and bottom-to-top.
  • Maximum reached: further credit edges do not change the count or write beyond the last slot.
  • Start consumes one credit and enters level start; no timeout restarts attract before a game is played.

Options and recommendation

Recommended: add non-rendering named coin-location objects to the existing TMX slot origins, then have the compiler sort them left-to-right within rows and bottom-to-top across rows. This keeps the authored screen authoritative and lets the build reject missing, duplicate, clipped, or reordered slots. Retaining source constants is rejected because the current mismatch demonstrates that they can diverge from the TMX.

Discriminating test

Generate the ordered destination list from the TMX coin-location metadata, reject duplicates and off-screen addresses, and force every credit count from zero through maximum while comparing decoded framebuffer cells. Separately trace the presentation state sequence from boot, credit, start, game over, and optional high-score handoff.

Acceptance criteria

  • The generated destination list exactly equals all approved TMX coin-location objects in left-to-right, bottom-to-top order.
  • For each credit count, exactly that many complete coin glyphs are visible and no pixels are clipped or written outside the authored slots.
  • The high-score screen is entered only by a new credit edge before gameplay and is not part of unattended boot rotation.
  • Credits saturate at the number of authored slots; start consumes exactly one.
  • Attract resumes only after game over and the applicable FEAT-003 handoff, not from a credit-screen timeout.
  • Cold payload, module, combined, framebuffer, and future-sound capacity checks remain passing.
