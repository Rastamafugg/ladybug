Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Cold and warm cartridge launch must publish coco-attract-screen.tmx first. The high-score screen is a credit-triggered state and must not appear during unattended boot.

Reproduction

  1. Launch the cartridge without pressing a key.
  2. Observe the first fully published presentation screen.
  3. Continue watching until the attract title appears later in the rotation.

Observed versus expected

Observed: the high-score screen is first; the proper attract screen appears as the next state. This is one user observation. Source initializes PRES_SCREEN through start_screen(PRESENTATION_MAP_ATTRACT), so the mismatch may be state selection, map-index generation, stream resolution, or publication order; none is yet confirmed.

Expected: attract is the first visible screen after load. High score is entered only by a new 5 or 6 edge.

Scope

In: cold/warm state initialization, map identifiers and offsets, selected-screen stream resolution, and first-frame publication. Out: title actor choreography, coin layout, high-score records, and gameplay initialization.

Discriminating test

At the first screen request and first publication, capture PRES_SCREEN, PRES_MODE, selected stream offset/hash, and final framebuffer hash. Require the requested attract stream and compare it byte-for-byte with the compiled attract TMX output.

Acceptance criteria

  • Cold launch and warm reset publish the attract TMX as the first complete visible screen without input.
  • No high-score map load or publication occurs before a valid credit edge.
  • The attract stream identifier, encoded hash, decoded cells, and published framebuffer agree.
  • The natural attract rotation still advances to instructions and the remaining configured states.

Confirmed cause and current evidence

A bounded XRoar/GDB capture at checkpoint 8183005 proved that the first call requests attract map 0, then cold initialization replaces the actual idle samples retained by scan_keys with $FF,$FF,$FF. The next idle scan reads $7F,$7F,$7F, synthesizes credit event mask $07, requests high-score map 3, and completes that load first. The authored and live low-RAM presentation modules match byte-for-byte at 1,226 bytes with SHA-256 864ef67ea03a58ba94c0f1f09307ad0f3c8624e57c333c8045a4e20433290015, excluding loader or relocation corruption. The first completed 30,720-byte framebuffer has SHA-256 04c5a2685745fd8f69647483448f48bea27248754caf3ffb9c7c0a882e992320, exactly matching compiled high score and not compiled attract 54c4aa78520e1726c41912a2ed4913d9be06c3b64b4ad279f52f201ad6f7c4f6. The attract encoded stream begins at cold offset 6,517, is 224 bytes, and has SHA-256 16779a13b9411f403b35b4db5b240ad5cca4653a5d55552d66df6988a00b715b; its decoded 960-cell hash is ffdf47330a3ce2dd1d5c13ecaa5f774fedac59e16ee435cf422e57bff7df3b42.
