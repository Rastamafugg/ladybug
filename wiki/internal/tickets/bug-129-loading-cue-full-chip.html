Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Fix the new-ladybug entry music (Cue 6, level-start) so the CoCo 3's single Games Master Cartridge (GMC) SN76489 sound chip plays all three distinct musical parts of the original arcade arrangement (lead melody, inner harmony, and bass) without missing the lead line or losing the bass mid-cue. The current ROM drops the lead melody and duplicates the bass across two channels, causing both missing melody and destructive phase cancellation on the second half of the cue.

Evidence and baseline

Read-only source and asset diagnosis performed on 2026-11-22 against repository main commit d2c2a0e9 (audio assets identical on codex/feat013-shared-hud commit 4f5fd30f):

  • Arcade Z80 source truth (assets/arcade/audio/z80-sound-engine.json): Cue 6 (descriptor_address: 7077) launches 4 tone streams (0 noise streams), each exactly 192 ticks (3.20 s at 60 Hz), across the arcade board's two 4 MHz SN76489 chips:
    • Stream 0 (4145 / $1031, 27 commands): Lead melody — highest and loudest voice (attenuation 2 then 1): B4–A4–B4–A4–B4–C#5–D5–C#5–(rest)–C#5–B4–C#5–D5–E5 (raw_sha256: 482fe500...).
    • Stream 1 (4200 / $1068, 27 commands): Inner harmony a third below (attenuation 4): G4–F#4–G4–F#4–G4–A4–B4–A4–(rest)–A4–G#4–A4–B4–C#5 (raw_sha256: aec4257e...).
    • Stream 2 (4255 / $109F, 20 commands): Rhythmic bass (attenuation 4): G3 (ticks 0–95) then A3 (ticks 96–191) with 2-tick rests (raw_sha256: d09da7db...).
    • Stream 3 (4296 / $10C8, 20 commands): Byte-for-byte identical duplicate of Stream 2 (same raw_sha256: d09da7db8da8dc88fdb94f9146634437f1b60c10bf9b80971dcbb5442b0e5af3).
    All other 17 cues use 1 or 2 streams; Cue 6 is the only cue exceeding 3 source streams.
  • Confirmed data-reduction defect (assets/arcade/audio/gmc-cue-policy.json, gmc-runtime-cues.json, gmc_sound_data.inc): The automated reduction method (drop-lowest-isolated-dual-reference-rms) selected selected_stream_indices: [1, 2, 3] and dropped Stream 0 (4145, the lead melody) because its raw isolated RMS error was lowest (2116.22 vs. 2842.04, 2954.37, 2975.18). As a result, the shipped CoCo stream plays only Harmony + Bass + Duplicate Bass.
  • Observed offline phase-cancellation reproduction (repro/bug129-cue06-attenuation-test/): In src/audio_runtime.s, audio_mix_clear resets muted physical channels to period = 1 (toggling their SN76489 polarity flip-flops at 125 kHz) and audio_mix_slot compacts active voices into physical channels 0..2. When Voice 0 (harmony) rests at ticks 106..108, the two identical bass voices shift from channels (1, 2) to (0, 1) while channel 2 mutes to period = 1, then shift back at tick 108. In an offline 4 MHz SN76489 divider simulation of the exact CoCo write sequence (single deterministic simulation, not live hardware capture), combined mix RMS drops from ~10,590 (ticks 0–106, both bass channels in phase) to ~4,880 across ticks 114–192 (almost identical to harmony alone at 4,777) because the two identical bass channels cancel out of phase.
  • Exact byte-size measurement (host-encoded, verified against existing SHA-256): Re-encoding [1, 2, 3] reproduces the existing 337-byte gmc_cue_stream_06 bit-for-bit (SHA-256: 7dcb9bd0e141b9b44c9a7c01c5afbe92a8c69fe97c358a067f1f0b6234db59d0). Encoding [0, 1, 2] (at either bass attenuation 4 or 2) produces 365 bytes — an exact delta of +28 B, moving total packed sound data from 3,478 B to 3,506 B. On FEAT-013's latest passing complete-profile build (9fbde1eb), page $3D holds audio ($A000–$BA0C), keybinding ($BA0C–$BE98), and the $BF00 word-painter helper ($BF00–$BF92), leaving a 104 B gap before $BF00 and 110 B after $BF92; +28 B in the audio image shifts keybinding end from $BE98 to $BEB4 (projected, leaving 76 B before $BF00), with zero overlap.
  • Ruled-out runtime defects (by static inspection): audio_mix_write emits valid 10-bit SN76489 latch/data/attenuation bytes on all three tone channels, and iet_final_wait in src/main.s holds player control until AUDIO_MUSIC_PUBLISHED_BUSY == 0, so gameplay does not cut Cue 6 off early.

Scope

In scope:

  • Change Cue 6's selected source streams in assets/arcade/audio/gmc-cue-policy.json from [1, 2, 3] (dropping Stream 0) to [0, 1, 2] (dropping duplicate Stream 3 at address 4296), applying the user-selected single-channel bass attenuation (4 or 2).
  • Regenerate Cue 6's entries in assets/arcade/audio/gmc-runtime-cues.json and assets/arcade/audio/gmc_sound_data.inc, updating subsequent stream binary offsets in the manifest while keeping all 17 other cue streams byte-identical.
  • Update PACKED_SOUND_BYTES in scripts/verify_audio_runtime.py from 3478 to 3506 and add/retain Cue 6 voice-selection validation so the existing audio verifier stays exact.
  • Complete-profile build, audio verifier check, capacity verification on page $3D, and user-owned audible verification on the rebuilt ROM.

Out of scope:

  • Cue-to-visual onset timing (tracked separately in BUG-128).
  • Changes to src/audio_runtime.s, src/adaptive_audio_service.s, mixer arbitration, channel-compaction logic, or the SN76489 stream opcode format.
  • Changes to cues 0–5 or 7–17, new-life entry animation timing, or legacy non-complete build profiles.
  • Hardware/Multi-Pak Interface dual-chip expansion.

Scenarios and edge cases

  • Normal new-life start (risk: missing melody / phase-cancelling bass): Starting a live game plays Cue 6 across all 192 ticks (3.20 s) with all three distinct voices (Lead on tone 0, Harmony on tone 1, Bass on tone 2) audible and stable from start through the final E5 + C#5 + A3 cadence.
  • Replacement life entry after death (risk: repeated admission / stale shadow state): Losing a life and entering with a replacement ladybug replays Cue 6 cleanly with the same three voices and completes before control release.
  • Mid-cue rest boundaries (risk: channel-compaction phase shift): At tick 106..108 (where Lead and Harmony rest while Bass continues) and ticks 160..168 (staccato bass rests near the end), only one channel carries Bass, so no second identical-period channel can destructively cancel it.
  • Adjacent cues regression (risk: offset shift corrupts cues 7–17): Updating Cue 6 from 337 B to 365 B shifts stream offsets for cues 7–17 in gmc-runtime-cues.json; every cue 0–5 and 7–17 must retain byte-identical stream payloads and pass verify_audio_runtime.py.
  • Page-$3D & GMC source capacity (risk: module overlap or overflow): Rebuilding the complete profile must confirm audio + keybinding + FEAT-013's $BF00 word-painter fit without overlap inside page $3D and within the 65,536-byte GMC image.

Options and recommendation

  • Option A — [0, 1, 2] with source bass attenuation 4 (–8 dB): Pure deduplication of Stream 3 with zero volume adjustment. Exact source command fidelity on all three retained streams; bass is ~3–6 dB quieter than the arcade's doubled bass.
  • Option B — [0, 1, 2] with boosted bass attenuation 2 (–4 dB): Drops duplicate Stream 3 and raises the single remaining bass channel by +4 dB (2 SN76489 steps) to approximate the arcade's doubled two-chip bass energy without overpowering the lead melody (attenuation 2 → 1).
  • Offline listening comparison prepared: Four 48 kHz WAV renders at a shared fixed voice gain are retained under repro/bug129-cue06-attenuation-test/ (cue06-1-current-coco-broken-123.wav, cue06-2-fixed-012-bass-att4.wav, cue06-3-fixed-012-bass-att2.wav, and cue06-4-arcade-4stream-ref.wav). The user listens and chooses attenuation 4 or 2 when approving this ticket.
  • Rejected alternatives: Keeping [1, 2, 3] leaves the lead melody missing; rewriting audio_mix_slot to preserve fixed physical-channel mapping would add runtime code and still not restore the dropped lead melody; requiring a second sound chip violates the single-GMC hardware target.
