BUG-004 — Exclude skulls from colour-cycle redraw Proposed

Keep skull collectibles white and outside bonus-colour redraw work.

Identity and ordering

  • Status: Proposed
  • Created / updated: 2026-08-06 / 2026-08-06
  • Priority / order: P1 / 6
  • Owner: unassigned
  • Dependency: BUG-003 approval/integration recommended first
  • Approval: required before implementation

Objective and evidence

Skull collectibles are authored as white/static objects and must not participate in the red/yellow/blue bonus-colour cycle. Current bonus_color_tick marks the entire entity set for redraw, and draw_entities traverses skull records even though draw_entity_object selects the static skull LUT. The visual symptom is white skulls plus the shared underlay artifact, which disappears during the first colour-cycle redraw.

Expected: skull pixels remain white across every colour transition, skull records are excluded from bonus-colour redraw work, and hearts/letters continue to recolour normally.

Scope and acceptance

In: colour-cycle entity selection, skull/static LUT handling, focused cycle captures, and regression evidence. Out: skull art, skull collision/death behavior, bonus timing, and stage-load underlay ordering except where BUG-003 integration is required. Acceptance requires all forced colour transitions to leave skull pixels unchanged while every bonus object changes according to BONUS_COLOR.

Decision log

  • 2026-08-06 — Proposed from user report; debugger confirmed skull rendering is static white and identified whole-entity redraw as the remaining scope. Awaiting explicit approval.

Source context: src/main.s bonus_color_tick, draw_entities, draw_entity_object, and object_skull_lut.
