BUG-062 — Render lives in the authored 12-slot HUD grid Proposed

2026-10-01 · P2 · High-risk · Proposed · RSCH-014 Step A

Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Use the twelve authored ladybug slots for selected lives, filling from the bottom-left across and upward, and removing in reverse order.

Evidence, reproduction and cause

The copied coco-screen.tmx already contains twelve complete 2×2 icon slots at roots x={33,35,37}, y={15,17,19,21}; the exact raw GIDs and 48 quadrant cells are in snapshot.json. In src/main.s, draw_lives loops only three roots at (33,21),(35,21),(37,21). Complete profile replaces markers with a numeric reserve count when LIVES>=4. FEAT-005 selects 1–12 total lives; BUG-039 defines the initial rightmost-marker transfer to the active player.

Scope

In: map the configured/remaining life units to all twelve existing icon roots, including exact fill/depletion order and initial transfer compatibility. Out: changing the selected total, death rules, extra-life awards, marker art or active-player animation except as required to preserve BUG-039’s handoff.

Design and prototype

Index slots from 0 through 11 by row: bottom row y21 left-to-right, then y19, y17 and y15. Draw the first N occupied slots. Depletion removes the highest occupied index, which is right-to-left on the topmost occupied row, then continues toward lower rows. Preserve FEAT-005 total-life meaning and BUG-039’s single rightmost active-player conversion; do not introduce a second counter.

Scenarios and edge cases

  • Initial configured totals 1, 3 and 12 display exactly that many markers at the ordered roots; all twelve fit without touching adjacent score/credit fields.
  • Initial active-player transfer consumes exactly the last-filled slot; subsequent deaths remove one marker at a time in reverse order, through zero reserves, without duplicate or stale marker pixels.
  • Natural new-game entry, controlled boundary counts, and both framebuffer-owner histories agree; an isolated A/B pass does not substitute for natural alternating publication.
  • No numeric fallback replaces the requested icons at 4–12. Existing life accounting, extra-life awards and stage persistence remain intact; complete build and capacity guards pass.

Cost class, dependencies and risk

Class: High-risk. Dependencies: FEAT-005 selected-life semantics and current map parser must integrate first. BUG-039 owns initial live-player handoff and death accounting; this ticket owns only indicator drawing/order. The old BUG-039 three-life visual pass is historical and does not cover 12 slots or current TMX bytes.

Risk controls: use only current-revision artifacts and exact authored target cells. A missing natural screen, build or edge scenario is incomplete evidence. Any change to outcome, behavior, dependencies or risk after approval returns this ticket to Proposed.

Retain marker-root/pixel summaries and per-owner hashes.

Evidence and commit: retain concise current-map/source/ROM hashes, exact target-cell or event receipts, failing evidence and capacity deltas. One bounded implementation commit after explicit approval and Ready; no push or ticket closure by an execution agent. Parent owns integration and QA.
