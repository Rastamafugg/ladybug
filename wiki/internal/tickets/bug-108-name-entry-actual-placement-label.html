Objective and confirmed source cause

Make the enter-high-score phrase read YOU GET TOP HI SCORE for first place and YOU GET 2ND HI SCORE, 3RD, 4TH, and the applicable ordinal for later placement. Baseline fe160ae389145d24083218ded05ba9cdca781474 authors the phrase statically; the active high-score name renderer updates names and scores but never writes a placement into this phrase. highscore_prepare_name stores the insertion rank in PRES_INSERT (0–8) before the name screen.

Scope and design

In: replace only the three-character TOP token at x1–3,y12 with TOP for rank1 or the correct ordinal for ranks2–9. Preserve HI, row10, row14, and all score/name fields. Use PRES_INSERT as the authoritative rank; do not recompute ranking or alter row order.

Scenarios and acceptance

  • Force first, second, third, fourth and final supported insertion slots.
  • Change from a longer ordinal to a shorter token on owner replay and check stale cells.
  • Verify the phrase remains within its authored row and neighboring TOP record fields are unchanged.

Acceptance requires the correct exact ordinal at the phrase location for every insertion rank and pixel-exact untouched surrounding text on both surfaces.
