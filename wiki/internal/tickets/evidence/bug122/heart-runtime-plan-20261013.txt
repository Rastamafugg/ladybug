Terminal input observer correction

The maintained source reassessment and src/keybinding_runtime.s establish different lifetimes for the two event bytes. key_scan clears/rebuilds PRES_EVENT at $A9 on every scan. It rewrites MENU_EVENT at $D0 only through ks_local when PRES_SCREEN is 3 or at least 6. On screen 2 it takes ks_done and may leave the previous $D0 edge, such as $10, while updating the menu-held history. Therefore at the released screen-2 level-preview boundary require $A9==0 and record $D0/$DF; do not require $D0==0 there and do not call it a product failure. Later, at qualified screen 3/mode 5, prove at least one key_scan service occurred with no key held, then require $D0==0. A nonzero $D0 before that screen-3 scan is expected stale state; a nonzero value after the proven scan is a separate input-service failure.

The terminal adapter already corrected the absent PRES_SCREEN-symbol assumption by using qualified direct-page $A6/$A7 reads. Its two prior runtime failures were verifier assumptions, not product acceptance failures: first “PRES_SCREEN map is None” despite valid direct-page identity; then the invalid screen-2 assertion observed PRES_EVENT=$00 and MENU_EVENT=$10. No further runtime attempt is authorized by this plan until the copied adapter applies the A9/D0 distinction and the parent reviews it. At screen 3/mode 5, still require credits, PRES_EVENT, MENU_EVENT, and MENU_SELECT neutral, screen publication stable, and mode-7 timer progression monotonic through at least 179 before menu publication. Then monitor mode 5 for 10 seconds; stable timer zero is expected. Do not wait for mode 2 after terminal death.
