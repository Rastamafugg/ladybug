RSCH-014 B7 — BUG-087 enemy rate design and fit

High-risk design preparation · 2026-10-02 · no production change

BUG-087 · Step D source policy · B5 owner and route audit · BUG-086 / PERF-008 current-fit receipt

Verdict

Keep BUG-087 Draft. The source policy can be represented by one shared roaming-enemy rate phase, but the bounded source candidate exceeds the enemy module boundary and its proposed direct-page bytes overlap committed presentation state. Arcade-to-CoCo cadence and later-part behavior also remain uncalibrated. No implementation or acceptance claim is ready.

Design boundary

The arcade ROM selects one shared movement step count for active roaming enemies from rate codes $10, $12, $15, and $18 on the default Easy/Medium table. Those codes average 1, 1.19921875, 1.5, and 1.796875 visible pixels per enemy dispatcher. The ROM uses a fractional accumulator, a $61B6 countdown initialized to 96 and reloaded to 60, and a $61B7 elapsed bucket that saturates at $F0. The rate index is min(15, part_offset + ($61B7 >> 4)). Step D places the first Part 1 Easy/Medium rate change at 5,796 logical gameplay-loop calls; this is not a raw-frame or CoCo-tick conversion.

The CoCo enemy movement routine emits two-pixel quanta. A parameterized proposal adds measured arcade pixel credit per admitted enemy simulation tick to one shared accumulator and emits one CoCo move per two accumulated pixels. It leaves BOX_TIMER, ENEMY_TIMER/ENEMY_ANIM, player cadence, LAST_FRAME, and adaptive debt policy independent. The one-arcade-dispatcher-per-CoCo-tick assumption is unproved. The 2-pixel quantum, rate-transition times, blocked/frozen behavior, and stage-wrap mapping therefore remain gates.

The parent-controlled quiet transition sample records all four enemies at two source-origin pixels per two logical ticks in both parts. Part 2 is not separately pixel-verified, the B5 route did not reach Part 2, and there is no later-part arcade enemy-rate capture. The parent’s 27,000-cycle target remains failing; B7 measured no candidate cycle cost.

Allocation result

The E2 AD1 keyboard baseline reports 55 bytes before fixed enemy LUT $17A0. The 87-byte BUG-086 helper occupies $A8A0-$A8F6, leaving six bytes before the reserved logo. The approved BUG-058 banked helper assembles to 696/698 bytes with SHA-256 3827719dd6d237bd994e072748ef9756a2b113872b897ab561574655d8f590ab; only two bytes remain in that helper window. Its direct assembly preserved the five reset/batch/append/reducer/complete entry points. The reducer branch displacement updates from 1600CF to 1600D5 as the target moves from $BE1F to $BE25; this follows the six-byte code shift. This was not a complete combined profile build.

The compact 8-bit fractional/parity source candidate fails the fixed enemy boundary: zmb $17A0-* is -143. That size-only candidate omits required $61B7 saturation, so it is an optimistic miss. A previous 8.8 draft failed with unsupported opcode aba and -91; it was discarded. The only full-build attempt ran bash scripts/build.sh build in WSL with inherited/default environment, stopped at enemy assembly with the 690-byte clock helper, and is not a fit pass.

The candidate’s six proposed state bytes at $DF-$E4 conflict with current source owners: PRES_NAME_ROW at $DF, name-entry bytes at $E0-$E3, and PRES_NAME_PTR at $E4-$E5. A mutually exclusive gameplay/name-entry alias would require lifecycle proof. The memory-map page’s generic “free” label is not sufficient to claim ownership.

Rejected alternatives and gates

Do not reuse the border timer or pose timer. The approved two-tick adaptive clock controls admitted simulation ticks and debt; it does not own enemy-rate selection. Per-enemy accumulators would introduce phase skew absent from the source’s shared step count. Remaining page-$34 helper slack cannot hold the rate routine, and no alternate code bank or loader mapping has proven physical ownership or delivery.

Keep BUG-087 Draft until arcade_timing_research closes movement-dispatch and elapsed-time conversion with later-part evidence, a physically owned source allocation fits the fixed boundary across required profiles, and direct-page/lifecycle ownership plus reset, spawn, blocked/frozen, death, and stage-wrap behavior are proven. The 27,000-cycle target remains a separate failing criterion. The machine-readable receipt beside this page contains commands, hashes, profile capacity, source identity limits, and verification details.
