Source facts that guide the discriminator

AD_WORK_EXEC dispatches input and audio service, attempts adaptive batching, and may compose a BACK framebuffer. Its active path can call AD_BATCH_EXEC for logical work; adaptive_render prepares the selected BACK owner, restores actor closure, reduces damage, and draws the closure. Audio services are separate calls. framebuffer_finish_back captures owner metadata and arms FB_RENDER_PENDING; the Vbord IRQ owns the actual display-offset/owner commit. The gross target interval can contain one or more IRQs, while the final commit may occur after AD_WORK_EXEC returns. Record readiness setup, any IRQ work inside the timed interval, and the first subsequent commit as separately labeled markers; preserve the approved gross interval and do not move its endpoint to make a result pass.
