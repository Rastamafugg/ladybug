Finding and limit

The helper/page contents and PAR5 mapping are correct. The confirmed local fault is the obsolete caller literal `$A463`: it enters the intact rate lookup table six bytes before the actual shim and executes table data against `PLAYER_FB`. This explains the captured logical/render-pointer disagreement and displaced BACK image in the demo run. It does not yet prove that the same call is the cause of the user's natural level-2 symptom, nor does it show the displaced pose in accepted FRONT. Do not treat BUG-123 as reproduced for its primary level-2 acceptance case yet.
