Disposition

Branch and ABI checks

Within the proposed name layout, the short displacements are: entry BNE nlt_snap +109; BNE nlt_have_steps +2; BEQ nlt_no -10; first-callback BEQ nlt_assist +50; computed-pair BNE nlt_assist +16; mask-loop BEQ/BNE +4/-4; old-edge BEQ nlt_assist_result +10; shared-result BNE nlt_no -102; direct-return BRA nlt_yes +42; direct BSR nlt_common_state +59; snap BSR nlt_vector +18; vector BSR nlt_delta +21/+13; and name BSR nlt_project_old_axis +55. These offsets follow the 175-byte layout above, with the eleven-byte common-state helper before nlt_vector. Each stays within signed 8-bit range. Gameplay LBSR calls are 16-bit relative; the pt_try local BNE/BEQ displacements are +6/+3, and the snap BRA retains the existing +$50 destination distance. The normal exact-flag lwasm build must still confirm all encodings.

Name direct safe turns still require the current requested edge and clear matching upper hazard bit. The 34-byte selector's clockwise index matches generator/verifier pair order NE=$10, ES=$20, SW=$40, WN=$80: `(PLAYER_DIR - PLAYER_WANT) & 3` is 3 clockwise and 1 counter-clockwise; select WANT for 1, otherwise PLAYER_DIR. The 18-byte old-edge loop maps N/E/S/W values 0/1/2/3 to low mask bits 1/2/4/8. The computed mask is ANDed with the saved graph B, then B is restored before either hold or projected lookup. No future-center snap hazard is tested.

A shared same/opposite/perpendicular classifier does not fit this budget. Its exact 20-byte body is: `LDA PLAYER_WANT` (2), `CMPA PLAYER_DIR` (2), `BEQ same` (2), `EORA #2` (2), `CMPA PLAYER_DIR` (2), `BEQ opposite` (2), `CLRA` (1), `RTS` (1), `same: LDA #1` (2), `RTS` (1), `opposite: LDA #2` (2), `RTS` (1). The name shim is 5 bytes (`BSR` 2, `TSTA` 1, `BNE nlt_no` 2); the game shim is 12 (`LBSR` 3, `TSTA` 1, `BEQ pt_try` 2, `DECA` 1, `BNE pt_reverse` 2, `LBRA pt_advance` 3). Together they replace the 8-byte name parity predicate and 14-byte game prefilter with 37 bytes, a net increase of 15; the joined area would be 259 bytes, 13 over its 246-byte ceiling. Even a smaller shared XOR-return helper is not profitable: a 5-byte `LDA WANT / EORA DIR / RTS` body plus a 6-byte name shim and 15-byte game shim totals 26, versus the existing 22, producing 248 bytes, two over ceiling. Keep the local name parity predicate and game triage because their outcome routing differs; consolidate their common no/return outcomes within the name routine instead. The shared direct-state helper is profitable in the combined measured area: its 11-byte body and 2-/3-byte calls plus the 16-/12-byte adapters total 39 versus the measured 41-byte direct paths, saving two. This is the lowest-cost encoded join found without changing gate ownership or callback ABI.

Disposition

The joined resident source sketch accounts for the previously missing recovery and fits the unchanged arithmetic ceiling by two bytes. Relative to the 291-byte fragment projection it saves 47 bytes, exceeding the 45-byte join requirement. This closes the source-design feasibility step only. Before candidate implementation, parent review should decide whether the two-byte projected resident margin is sufficient to proceed to the already-required normal three-profile builds and geometry oracle. Any branch expansion, copied-name cap miss, helper-end miss, or profile free-space result below 32 remains a failure. No recovery beyond the approved files, source regions, or mapping is proposed.
