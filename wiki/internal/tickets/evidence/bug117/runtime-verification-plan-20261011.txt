Required phases and markers
---------------------------
1. **Delivery identity — 45 seconds.** Success: current ROM identity and all required resident/staged/live bytes match; current maps resolve every watched routine and destination. Timeout means the identity/control boundary was not reached, not that game code is slow. Any mismatch stops the scenario before symbol-based interpretation.
2. **Natural life chain — each named wait at most 45 seconds, monitor stop at most 10 seconds.** Cold boot, credit, and start through the ordinary input path. After monitor-side byte qualification, arm dynamic state-2/audio/publication watches before the first cue service; do not wait for the existing full-entry startup marker. Run the initial life and two genuine enemy-contact deaths/replacements in one natural progression so the initial plus two replacement marker columns cover x=33/35/37 by life index. Do not write life count or entry state to manufacture a pass. Require exactly one life decrement per real death and one cue-6 admission per entry. Record the marker column, `DEATH_TIMER` before the first HUD render, entry-state edges, every route pose, logical/frame sample, offscreen pad ticks, first visible maze frame, and release. Record both nominal logical-event alignment and actual cue-relative first-visible frame/event timing. Count equality alone does not establish visible synchronization, and no new tolerance is silently approved; departure lengths and added invisible padding must reconcile without skipping any two-pixel pose.
3. **Cue-first and automatic-control gates — same natural chain.** At cue-6 admission, record its slot/stream identity, then identify the first cue-6-owned tone/output state from the same service's mixed stream. Stop at the actual `SND_DATA` store and record its value/register and service order. An earlier credit/effect `audio_write`, enqueue, slot-0 ID 6, or busy flag is not cue-6 audible evidence. Require the cue-6-owned write before the first entrant is published in FRONT. Hold an ordinary direction through each automatic entry. From onset through release, require no accepted player-control change or input-caused deviation from the automatic route/facing, no contact/death transition, and no new cue-13 enqueue; a cue 13 admitted by the preceding real death is recorded separately as prior music.
4. **Prior-music and completion-order gates — separate forced runs, same representative integrated build.** Force a qualified pre-entry active/queued music item and prove the hidden state-1 wait drains it through ordinary audio service before opening the cue-6 animation edge. Absence of an active prior item means this scenario is absent and fails. Force and separately receipt both completion orders: music terminal output before final-pose commit, and final-pose commit before music terminal output. Use existing scheduler/audio/IRQ paths and observer-side event ordinals; do not write game state, frame clocks, framebuffer ownership, or saved sequence data to force a result. In either order, require release only after (a) cue-6's actual final PSG output and shared post-PSG tail publish idle, and (b) the final pose is found in the actual FRONT image after IRQ/owner commit. `Y=18`, render-request set, pending clear, or busy=0 alone is not a pass. If either order cannot be forced through valid paths, record it missing and stop acceptance.
5. **Rendering regression — focused, no full cross-product.** Use one representative complete build. The fit/static contract already checks all 24 row-address identities. Runtime compares top/bottom row destinations and rendered pixels through each isolated framebuffer owner, then verifies the natural life chain's alternating FRONT images. Compare part/vegetable HUD output at stages 1, 10, 100, and 255 against the hash-pinned pre-BUG-117 baseline, including fixed coordinates and player initialization state. Do not repeat every HUD value for every owner or every profile.
6. **Independent timing gate — 45 seconds per worklist.** Re-measure the existing required worklists on the integrated source. Success requires each required worklist to complete and meet 27000 cycles. If measurement does not return in 45 seconds, record that probe boundary and the last proven marker; do not infer that target code is slow. The retained 42541/54539-cycle maxima remain a failure; this behavior plan does not waive or claim to fix them.
