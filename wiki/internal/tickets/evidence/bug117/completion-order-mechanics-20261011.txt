BUG-117 completion-order mechanics — source-only review

Scope: inspect existing candidate/control sources and classify legal ways to
exercise the two required completion orders. No build, observer import,

Decision
--------
The current approved controls do not provide a deterministic way to force
either completion order independently, and therefore cannot guarantee both.
Keep both required orders as an unresolved acceptance gate. Natural runs can
show which order the ordinary candidate produces; an observed natural order is
useful evidence, but is not proof that both required forced scenarios passed.
No missing order may be waived or inferred from a plan.

What existing execution can vary
-------------------------------
An in-scope diagnostic attempt can use ordinary credit/start and two genuine
enemy-contact deaths/replacements, with monitor input only for normal gameplay
and no input accepted during automated entry. This naturally reaches the three
life-marker columns. `marker_entry_begin` chooses its HUD start from the current
life count and seeds the existing 80-tick pad (`main.s:905-920`); that legal
route-length variation may change whether cue 6 or the final FRONT commit
arrives first. It is a bounded natural-order observation, not a force: the
source does not establish that the three columns straddle both outcomes.

A real pre-entry music item can be produced through normal gameplay, then
allowed to drain through audio service before a replacement entry. The source
puts death/replacement in `ENTRY_WAIT_STAGE` while published music is busy and
only opens the entry after it becomes idle (`main.s:901-905, 4253-4262`). This
is suitable for the separate prior-music-drain case; it cannot independently
extend the newly admitted cue 6 after entry starts.

The audio and entrant share ordinary foreground progression. Nonadaptive entry
does its entry tick, audio service, and render in one main-loop path
(`main.s:768-783`); adaptive work runs elapsed-audio service before the logical
batch, and entry uses the same scheduler tick (`adaptive_active.s:22-29,
150-171`; `adaptive_audio_service.s:15-21, 82-111`). VBlank IRQ publication is
the only FRONT publication path (source contract in `main.s:43`). A legal
order receipt must distinguish:

* `music-first`: identify cue-6-owned terminal tone/output from the same mix
  service, observe its actual `SND_DATA` store, run through the shared
  `audio_mix_write_done` tail and busy publication, then observe the final
  entrant pose actually committed in FRONT by the IRQ.
* `front-first`: observe that same actual final-pose FRONT commit while the
  published music-busy state is still set, then observe cue 6's terminal
  output, shared post-write tail, and published idle state. Only after both
  commit and tail proofs may entry release count as correct.

Enqueue/slot-0 ID 6, the first arbitrary `audio_write` (which may be a credit
or effect), `PLAYER_CELL_Y=18`, a render request, cleared pending, or busy=0
alone is not either order marker. `audio_mix_write` calls `audio_write` for
actual PSG stores and `audio_mix_write_done` calls `audio_music_busy` before
publishing `AUDIO_MUSIC_PUBLISHED_BUSY` (`audio_runtime.s:693-780`). The future
observer must resolve labels/addresses from the integrated build maps and
qualify authored, staged, and live bytes before interpreting breakpoints.

Why the present observer cannot force the orders
------------------------------------------------
The XRoar monitor exposes run/pause, instruction-step, breakpoints/watchpoints,
read-only memory/register/cycle reads, and keyboard injection. `pause` changes
the emulated CPU run state to halted; `step_instruction` is accepted only
while halted and executes a bounded count of that same CPU's instructions
(`docs/reference/xroar/src/monitor.c:508-559, 827-854, 1308-1330`). It has no
control that advances audio/IRQ time while holding entry simulation, or holds
audio while allowing the entry/IRQ path to proceed. Breakpoints and watchpoints
observe normal execution; they do not schedule an independent owner clock.
`inject_key` only queues ordinary input (`monitor.c:1606-1638`), and
`read_cycles` only samples the shared event clock (`monitor.c:847-854`). The
wiki also prohibits simultaneous GDB and monitor control (`wiki/internal/tooling/xroar.html:160`).

The shared `Run.go` helper uses the monitor's whole-CPU run/stop boundary and
contains memory-write helpers (`repro/bugpass-20261010/luna122-qa-plan/runtime.py:10-14, 17-18`). Its current audio watch only records enqueue IDs 3/6 at
`audio_enqueue_impl` (`runtime.py:194-220`), while `Run.start` waits for state 4
and `trace_entry` samples only while state 4 (`runtime.py:246-275`). Neither
proves state-2 onset, cue-13 absence, cue-6-owned PSG output, terminal tail,
actual FRONT pixels, nor either completion order. It needs a narrow observer
adaptation before a later authorized execution. The corrected guarded
BUG-121 adapter exists at
`repro/bug122-parent/luna121-replacement-qa/verify_replacement.py`; it does not
add independent scheduler/audio control. `repro/bugpass-20261010/observe.py`
launches XRoar at import time and is not safe to import. No helper was imported
or executed here.

Allowed and disallowed alternatives
-----------------------------------
Within current scope: add observation-only event ordinals and use the legal
natural life-index/marker-column variation above to determine which order the
current scheduler produces. Name separate order-attempt phases, each with a
45-second hard deadline and monitor stop waits no longer than 10 seconds. The
success marker is the complete ordered evidence defined above. Timeout means
that this legal scenario did not produce its required order before the probe
boundary; it does not prove target code is slow. A natural result may guide
the next discriminating decision, but cannot replace the required forced-order
acceptance.

Not available within this assignment: debugger pauses/steps as a timing shim,
synthetic key patterns during entry, cue/audio dwell edits, writes to clocks,
entry state, owner/sequence or framebuffer control bytes, or direct audio
service invocation. These either halt all emulated progress, are ignored by
the automatic-entry owner, alter state outside ordinary game paths, or bypass
the tested scheduler. A selective audio/clock gate or a game-side test seam
would add a new control/ownership mechanism; it also crosses the approved
no-protocol/no-control-write boundary and needs a changed design/verification
approval. Replacing “force both” with “observe both if naturally encountered”
would relax the accepted criterion and also needs renewed approval. Do not
extend the deferred monitor protocol as part of this ticket.

Remaining Ready execution gate
------------------------------
After measured fit and parent integration, first pin the combined build/maps
and prove staged/live bytes. A guarded adapter may then attempt the natural
legal variations and record event order. Unless both distinct forced-order
receipts are obtained using an approved legal mechanism, report the missing
order and stop BUG-117 acceptance. No order was observed or passed in this
source-only planning step.
