Confirmed source behavior

The generated `assets/arcade/audio/gmc_sound_data.inc` table has 8-byte descriptors. `audio_admit` indexes an entry by cue ID times eight, stores descriptor byte 0 as slot policy, derives slot priority from its top three bits, uses descriptor byte 2 bit 7 to route music versus effect admission, and loads the big-endian stream pointer from bytes 4 and 5. Cue 3's current descriptor begins `8A 08 20 FC`, points to `gmc_cue_stream_03`, and ends `08 00`; cue 13 is the existing exclusive music descriptor `D3 A0 E0 1F` plus its stream pointer and trailing fields. The full descriptor must be compared as a unit; this plan does not assign unverified meaning to every descriptor field.

Cue 3's authored stream is `1C 06 01 02 04 1C 04 01 02 04 1D 0F 02 04 1E 08 00`. In the captured slot, the cursor is after the second WAIT with two ticks remaining. Replaying that prefix yields default tone periods and muted tone attenuations, with noise value 4 and attenuation 1, matching the captured slot.

In `src/audio_runtime.s`, `audio_mix_find_exclusive` considers active slots whose policy bit 0 is set, selects the greatest priority, calls `audio_mix_slot` only for that slot, then returns. Cue 13's $D3 policy is exclusive; cue 3's $8A policy is not. Thus cue 3 remains active in its effect slot but does not contribute to this exclusive cue-13 mixer service. This interpretation is independently consistent with the actual mixer buffers and pure-model output in the retained snapshot.
