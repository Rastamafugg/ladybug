BUG-060 — Separate base/background and maze-border tile ownership Proposed

2026-10-01 · P2 · Standard · Proposed · RSCH-014 Step A

Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Make each screen map assign blank/background cells to its base visual role and maze-border art to the border role, while preserving every composed screen pixel.

Evidence, reproduction and cause

The current copied snapshot still mixes those roles. coco-screen.tmx has 497 blank GID8 cells and its border in one 960-cell Maze and Panel Background layer. The three Arcade Maze Border maps each contain 825 GID8 blank cells plus 135 nonblank cells; Level Start Panel has 767 blanks plus 193 nonblank cells. These are current authored-layer counts, not historical pass claims. snapshot.json records all nine map hashes and layer counts.

Scope

In: role-aware separation of base/blank/background, maze-border and screen-specific panel content across maps that contain a maze border; preserve current GID transforms, overlays and ordering. Resolve literal layer names through BUG-059’s shared schema. Out: re-drawing art, changing screen content/palette, inventing a gameplay-only naming scheme, or altering unrelated menu and runtime data.

Design and prototype

An in-memory gameplay-map prototype extracts the 92-cell geometric outer ring into a border overlay, substitutes the authored blank GID8 beneath it, and recomposes all 960 GIDs exactly. It writes no TMX. Other border-role maps require role-aware classification: preserve the 43 nonblank non-ring cells in the current border layer until proven to be background; for level-start, keep the central panel cells separate from the ring. Do not infer border from “nonblank” alone.

Scenarios and edge cases

  • For every affected map, background/blank records appear only in the agreed base role; every border record belongs to the agreed border role; screen-specific panel content remains in its own role.
  • Recomposing layers in declared order yields exactly the same 40×24 tile map and static packed-pen frame as the current user-authored map for all cells not covered by separately approved color/layout tickets.
  • All blank, border, HUD, screen-overlay, sprite metadata and deferred-logo cells have one documented owner; absent or duplicate owners fail.
  • Current and candidate static-frame hashes match per screen; normal presentation and complete build/layer guards pass once BUG-059/FEAT-005 dependencies are integrated.

Cost class, dependencies and risk

Class: Standard. Dependencies: BUG-059 defines canonical role names and strict content checks. BUG-063/064 supply authored static-screen baselines; BUG-065/066/068/069 own their exact palette cells and are not folded into this layer move. Historical BUG-035/048 are reference only.

Risk controls: use only current-revision artifacts and exact authored target cells. A missing natural screen, build or edge scenario is incomplete evidence. Any change to outcome, behavior, dependencies or risk after approval returns this ticket to Proposed.

Assignment and verification contract

Owned artifacts after approval: The exact affected TMX maps are selected by role after BUG-059 is approved. Candidate files are tiled/coco-screen.tmx, tiled/coco-instructions-screen.tmx, tiled/coco-enter-high-score-screen.tmx, tiled/coco-game-over-screen.tmx and tiled/coco-level-start-screen.tmx, plus scripts/build_screen.py, scripts/build_presentation.py and existing layer verification. Their current bytes are immutable inputs during this preparation.

Required commands and scenarios: python wiki/internal/tickets/evidence/rsch014-A/capture_evidence.py; python scripts/verify_bug016_presentation_layers.py --tiled-dir tiled; complete-profile scripts/build.sh build after the approved layer schema is integrated. Require a per-map composed-map and packed-pen hash comparison.
