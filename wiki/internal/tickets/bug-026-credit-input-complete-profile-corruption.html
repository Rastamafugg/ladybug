Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Capture the first unexpected PC or state after a natural key-5 credit edge, prove the relevant live bytes against the built complete artifact, and reduce the corruption to one discriminating cause. Do not implement a fix until the cause is confirmed and this ticket is explicitly approved and prepared as Ready.

Reproduction and retained evidence

Build the complete profile from WSL, then launch /usr/local/bin/xroar -machine coco3 -ram 512 -cart-type gmc -cart-rom build/ladybug.rom -cart-autorun -ao pulse -joy-right kjoy0. The 2026-08-31 clean visible run used no GDB and no emulated memory writes. Pressing 5 once to add a credit changed the display to full-frame multicolour garbage with a white border. XRoar PID 2437 remained alive in state Ssl+; therefore the observation is an emulated crash or corrupted/frozen presentation, not an emulator-process exit. Windows Graphics Capture retained a 531-by-425 observation of the failed XRoar window. The user reports one similar earlier key-5 crash, but that historical run has no PC or state capture. Concise run metadata is retained in build/bug026-current-crash-evidence.json.

Observed versus expected

Observed: a natural credit key edge corrupts the active image and border while the emulator host stays alive. Expected: credit count and presentation advance through the authored credit/start path without framebuffer corruption, CPU loss, frozen output, or control loss.

Competing causes and discriminator

  • Runtime-owner candidate: the credit event or screen-start dispatch calls $0300/$0303 while the wrong auxiliary or audio owner is installed.
  • Mapping candidate: presentation, audio installation, or screen hydration uses a stale PAR5 mapping and reads or writes the wrong physical page.
  • Stack/return candidate: the audio installer/init tail return or presentation dispatch returns through a damaged stack.
  • PIA candidate: keyboard scan and GMC/right-joystick selector ownership interact at the credit edge.
  • Build/delivery candidate: lower likelihood because the launch hash was captured immediately before the run, but live bytes still must prove the executing artifact.

The discriminating probe is one clean GDB-enabled launch with bank-qualified breakpoints at credit scan, presentation-event publication, screen-start dispatch, runtime-owner installation, audio init/cue calls, and the first unexpected PC or state. Before interpreting any symbol, compare authored/staged/destination bytes for the active owner. The phase deadline is 60 seconds from the natural key-5 edge; timeout means the probe failed to bound the edge and does not exonerate the ROM.

Scope and exclusions

In scope: one bounded reproduction, exact command/hash/breakpoint/write/deadline evidence, live owner and PAR/stack validation, confirmed cause, the smallest cause-specific repair proposal, and synchronized ticket/wiki evidence. Out of scope: forcing presentation mode, screen selectors, death state, or timers as acceptance; unrelated audio cue redesign; web/monitor work; ownership or protocol changes without renewed approval; unrelated dirty-worktree changes; and committing failed experiments or temporary captures.

Smallest sufficient verification

  • Natural key-5 edge under GDB: covers the reported credit transition and captures the first invalid boundary.
  • Clean no-GDB key-5 replay after any approved repair: covers debugger-induced timing/control risk and requires intact credit/start presentation.
  • Right-joystick plus first audible cue: covers the shared PIA selector boundary without multiplying presentation modes.
  • FEAT-006 natural lifecycle verifier: remains the integration gate after BUG-026 is resolved; missing rare paths remain failures.

Acceptance criteria

  • The current complete artifact and active runtime owner are byte-identical at every symbol interpreted by the diagnosis.
  • A natural key-5 edge reaches the intended credit/start state without unexpected PC, stack damage, mapping error, framebuffer corruption, white-border failure, or frozen presentation.
  • The confirmed cause is supported by a discriminating trace; no cause is inferred from host-process liveness.
  • Any repair is limited to confirmed BUG-026/FEAT-006 scope and preserves the complete build, right joystick, audio route, resident/audio capacity limits, and 2,048-byte GMC source-margin target.
  • The clean visible replay uses -joy-right kjoy0, no GDB, no memory writes, and user-visible confirmation.

Assignment contract

Stop after one timeout, any discovery that expands the affected subsystem, or before the estimated 2% diagnostic share is exceeded; return to project management before another distinct probe.
