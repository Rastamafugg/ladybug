Objective, baseline and motivation

After the approved BUG-047 static logo recolour, the high-score logo must alternate the same two authored frames as the attract logo while preserving the blue logo and red R. Current pre-fix complete ROM SHA-256 57c7ceaeee735cb005c87441d2a09ef40c040c9764d3375e7d11ab8385ef50cd publishes a white static high-score logo on natural credit entry. The current attract bundle already holds both composed frames. Composed pixel evidence shows identical nonblack masks over 138 corresponding cells after a six-row shift, with twelve colour-changing cells across phases.

Proposed behavior and boundaries

On natural attract to accepted-credit high-score transition, initialize the high-score phase and publish the two existing logo frames at the attract cadence on both persistent framebuffer owners. Preserve static blue/R colours from BUG-047, table/prompt/coin/count authority from BUG-051/043/013, credit pre-emption and phase-selected auxiliary ownership. Scope excludes new glyphs, artist edits, regular actor animation, arcade speed retiming, audio and rank logic. A user-visible cold attract, credit edge, first high-score frame and two successive logo phases is the golden path. Alternate owner publication and a credit edge during a phase change are the adjacent replay cases.

Retain current ROM identity, frame hashes/pens, code size and a separate implementation commit. A missing phase or overflow fails. Dependency: BUG-047 static palette first. No implementation or approval is inferred from this proposal.
