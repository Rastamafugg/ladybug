Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Remove the reported lag between audio cues and visual triggers. For enemy release, start its cue before release and have it end at the release moment.

Evidence and baseline

User reports tangible onset lag across cues and gives enemy release as a case. No ROM, cue trace, audio capture, or frame tolerance was supplied. BUG-014 covers enemy release behavior, not cue timing; BUG-037 functional handoff is Done and timing work transferred to BUG-058.

Scope

In: cue-to-visual onset timing, including enemy release. Out: cue content, movement/release timing, unrelated gameplay rules, and general emulator performance.

Scenarios and edge cases

Reported audio starts after its visual trigger. For enemy release, requested behavior is cue start before the enemy appears and completion at that event. Cause and cue set are unknown.

Options and recommendation

Capture enqueue, first audio output, and visual markers for a representative cue and enemy release; determine whether delay is shared or cue-specific.

Acceptance criteria

Each approved cue starts within a user-approved tolerance of its visual trigger. Release cue begins before the enemy appears and ends at that event. Enemy release behavior stays unchanged.
