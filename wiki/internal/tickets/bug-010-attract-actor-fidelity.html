Approval brief

Objective and motivation

The attract title must show the complete arcade-referenced Lady Bug and enemy actor sequence using shared gameplay sprite services.

Reproduction

  1. Allow boot to reach the attract title.
  2. Observe the actors for a complete title-state duration.
  3. Compare actor count, identity, coordinates, movement, frames, and cadence with retained arcade captures.

Observed versus expected

Observed: only Lady Bug and one enemy are visible, and their animation does not appear correct. Current source defines one player destination and one enemy destination, so it cannot encode a larger arcade actor set.

Expected: every actor visible in the selected arcade reference sequence appears at capture-backed coordinates and follows the captured frame and timing sequence.

Scope

In: capture-backed actor inventory, coordinate transform, frame sequence, duration records, shared sparse drawing, and underlay restoration. Out: title map selection, instruction actors, new sprite art unless a capture proves an absent frame, and independent presentation movement/rendering engines.

Discriminating test

Extract an actor-by-frame oracle from the retained arcade capture, then compare each published title frame against actor identity, transformed coordinates, sparse frame index, and duration. A static source-label check is insufficient.

Acceptance criteria

  • The actor inventory and appearance order match the approved arcade reference interval.
  • Coordinates are capture-backed and transformed reproducibly into CoCo screen coordinates.
  • Animation frame changes and movement cadence match the reference within one 60 Hz presentation tick after documented frame-rate conversion.
  • Each previous actor footprint is restored and no static title cells are damaged.
  • No second actor renderer or cold sprite payload is introduced.

Assignment contract

Approved objective; unassigned pending selection of the exact arcade capture interval and a complete Ready contract. Expected artifacts are the retained arcade reference data, src/presentation_runtime.s, focused visual verification, this ticket, index, and log.

Verification and completion

Pending a frame-indexed arcade oracle, natural title capture, pixel comparison, and capacity report.
