Approval brief

Objective and baseline

At gameplay-source revision 82e1524, the measured worklist maxima were: horizontal four-enemy movement 28,763 active cycles, recorded while rendering into A; vertical four-enemy movement 27,887, recorded into B; four forced discontinuity captures 28,829, recorded into B; and natural nest-animation replay 28,222, recorded into B. The framebuffer targets are secondary metadata and are not offered as a causal explanation. Reduce horizontal movement to at most 25,000, requiring 3,763 cycles from its captured maximum. Keep vertical movement, forced discontinuity, and natural replay at or below 27,000, requiring 887, 1,829, and 1,222 cycles from their captured maxima.

Work and recommendation

Refresh attribution after dependencies, label each interval by scenario phase and executed worklist, then reduce shared restore, strip/full capture, sparse decode, and ownership orchestration. Treat natural replay and forced fallback as first-class scenarios rather than extrapolating from isolated buffer targets.

Scope

In: common actor closure, circular save-under restore/capture, decoder/orchestration, and replay overhead. Out: new gameplay behavior, graphics changes, and weakening conservative full-capture correctness.

Required scenarios

Horizontal and vertical motion; all relevant ring phases; arrivals, turns, and junctions; exactly four forced full captures; natural current-to-replay sequence; player-only regression coverage; both framebuffer targets; and reversed starting owner for controlled worklists.

Acceptance

Horizontal worklists are at most 25,000 and every other named scenario-phase/worklist is at most 27,000 regardless of framebuffer target; zero FRONT writes and missed commits occur on accepted scenarios; A/B convergence, pixel/save-under proofs, current-revision evidence, and all capacity guards pass.

Assignment contract

Not ready. After approval and dependency integration, refresh attribution, choose a bounded design, specify exact owned files and commands, retain distributions and rare-path evidence, use a separate commit, and report rejected experiments and capacity effects.
