Approval brief

Objective and baseline

Reduce horizontal four-enemy movement from a 28,763-cycle maximum to at most 25,000, requiring 3,763 cycles. Keep vertical movement at or below 27,000 from 27,887, forced discontinuity at or below 27,000 from 28,829, and natural nest-animation replay at or below 27,000 from 28,222.

Work and recommendation

Use current attribution to reduce shared restore, strip/full capture, sparse decode, and ownership orchestration. Treat natural replay and forced fallback as first-class scenarios rather than extrapolating from isolated owners.

Scope

In: common actor closure, circular save-under restore/capture, decoder/orchestration, and replay overhead. Out: new gameplay behavior, graphics changes, and weakening conservative full-capture correctness.

Required scenarios

Both owners; horizontal and vertical motion; all relevant ring phases; arrivals, turns, and junctions; exactly four forced full captures; natural current-to-replay sequence; and player-only regression coverage.

Acceptance

Horizontal is at most 25,000 and every other named scenario is at most 27,000; zero FRONT writes and missed commits occur on accepted scenarios; A/B convergence, pixel/save-under proofs, current-revision evidence, and all capacity guards pass.

Assignment contract

Not ready. After approval and dependency integration, refresh attribution, choose a bounded design, specify exact owned files and commands, retain distributions and rare-path evidence, use a separate commit, and report rejected experiments and capacity effects.
