  • Roaming enemies: the source documents eight initial type groups and adjacent-group selection after part eight. Forced MAME captures match sprite groups 11, 17, 29, 23, 35, 41, 47, and 53, but do not establish the dwell for every pose or direction.

  • Death, wings, and angel: death animation reference already records exact special-sequence intervals. Preserve those timings unless a new discrepancy is measured; do not convert them to the roaming timer.
  • Instruction and attract actors: instruction reference and attract actor reference contain phase-specific behavior. The current attract ROI is decorative evidence only.
  • Logo phases are separate in BUG-081. Collectible score popups already have a separate measured hold contract and are not actor locomotion.

Cause confidence and discrimination

Cause remains unconfirmed. Current CoCo evidence measures eight-tick player/enemy timer intervals and actual player publication holds of five to ten raw Vbords for one natural Part-1 sequence; this does not support simply lengthening either timer. Remaining causes include publication cadence, sprite-state selection, or family-specific arcade timing. Current arcade evidence has limited exact Part-1 player and roaming-enemy pose intervals, but the arcade animation clock owner is unproven and complete family coverage remains open. Measure contiguous pixel-state runs for each included family before changing a timer. Do not multiply type-by-direction samples unless source evidence shows independent direction clocks. Record displacement separately from pose transitions. See the current-versus-historical source-owner audit in B5 evidence.

Scope and acceptance

In: measured player and enemy pose dwell across the family inventory above, including any distinct roaming, den, release, or gameplay actor clock that source inspection confirms. Out: movement velocity, collision and AI rules, logo timing, popup display duration, broad scheduler slowdown, and already measured death art unless a conflicting measurement is obtained.

Acceptance requires a current arcade ROM/run identity, exact pose-state intervals, complete included-family coverage, preserved special-sequence references, and unchanged tile displacement at the accepted movement cadence. Missing required family or ambiguous state transitions fail evidence. CoCo cadence mapping must be measured, not inferred from MAME frame counts.

Class, recommendation, and dependencies

One current CoCo sequence maps eight scheduled pose ticks to raw Vbord publication samples; published player holds vary from five to ten Vbords, later settling at eight. Reclassify if the cause crosses into adaptive scheduling, memory, or framebuffer ownership.
