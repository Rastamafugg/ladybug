Approval brief

Objective and motivation

A credited Player 1 start must complete normal level-one construction and enter playable gameplay. This is a direct FEAT-002 acceptance failure and blocks closure.

Reproduction

  1. Launch the current combined cartridge.
  2. Press 5 or 6 once.
  3. Press 1.
  4. Wait for the level-start screen to hand off to gameplay.

Observed versus expected

Observed: the maze is drawn, then execution stalls before the nest enemy and collectibles are published. A bounded current-revision probe reproduces direct-page corruption and recursive renderer entry during the first stage frame.

Expected: the existing normal initialization path publishes the maze, gates, entities and collectibles, player, and nest enemy, then begins the ordinary gameplay loop.

Scope

In: preservation of the caller-owned destination register across indexed enemy/player stream resolution, presentation-to-live initialization order, first framebuffer publication, and bounded regression probes. Out: changes to maze data, enemy rules, collectible rules, two-player mode, presentation choreography, or a new renderer.

Confirmed cause and discriminating evidence

draw_enemy_stage enters with X=$1800. sparse_enemy_stream uses X as index scratch and does not restore it, so sparse_blit_stage enters with X=$0000. The sparse decoder then writes into direct page: ENTITY_COUNT changes from 8 to $CC, the return path is corrupted, and compose_enemy_zone re-enters frame_render_impl at $09D3 while the stack descends by two bytes per recursion. sparse_player_stream has the same register-clobber pattern, and both framebuffer consumers also require coverage. A GDB-only control that restores the caller destination at each sparse decoder reaches fbiq_publish with stack $1FF0, entity count 8, one active enemy, distinct front/back owners, pending commit 1, and front-write fault 0.

Acceptance criteria

  • A natural 5-then-1 sequence reaches controllable level-one gameplay within the existing level-start deadline.
  • The enemy is visible in the nest and the correct level collectibles replace their authored flowers before control begins.
  • Each initialization service returns once in the required order; no presentation-only substitute is added.
  • Enemy-stage, enemy-framebuffer, and player-framebuffer sparse decode preserve their caller-owned destination and complete within their declared destination ranges.
  • The shared demo initialization and first publication pass without BUG-008 corruption; BUG-012 retains ownership of any later natural-demo crash.
  • Subsequent-part startup, framebuffer ownership, capacity, and normal gameplay regression checks pass.
