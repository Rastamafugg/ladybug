Approval brief

Objective and motivation

The instruction screen must reproduce the arcade demonstration rather than use projected actor choices, positions, or timing. It must visibly teach the colour-gated EXTRA, SPECIAL, and heart multiplier rules.

Reproduction

  1. Allow attract mode to enter instructions without input.
  2. Observe every demonstration row through the transition to level start.
  3. Compare actors, row placement, colour sequence, pickup instant, highlighted labels, and bottom-row values with the arcade capture.

Observed versus expected

Observed: actor choices, placement, and timing are incorrect and too fast. The current implementation uses six fixed destinations and phase-derived colours, but no frame-by-frame arcade choreography oracle.

Expected: within the maze boundary, Lady Bug waits through a short, even red/yellow/blue cycle before collecting each item at its required colour: yellow for EXTRA, red for SPECIAL, and blue for the three hearts. Collection lights the matching EXTRA, SPECIAL, or multiplier characters. Heart collection appends the resulting multiplier to both bottom point-value rows. The collectible value shown in the bottom row changes with the active colour cycle.

Scope

In: arcade capture, actor identities and paths, in-maze coordinates, even colour timing, colour-gated pickups, label lighting, multiplier outcomes, bottom flower/collectible rows, changing collectible value, and actor underlay restoration. Out: changes to live-game bonus rules, new rendering engines, sound, and high-score presentation.

Scenarios and edge cases

  • EXTRA completes only on yellow and shows the extra-credit outcome.
  • SPECIAL completes only on red and shows the extra-life outcome.
  • Three-heart progression completes on blue and shows ×2, then ×3, then ×5.
  • Skull instruction behavior is captured and reproduced without applying the bonus colour redraw to a static skull.
  • Flower and collectible point rows remain legible; both receive the current multiplier after heart pickup, and the collectible value follows red/yellow/blue.
  • A credit or valid start pre-empts before the next actor or colour update without leaving a damaged underlay.

Discriminating test

Create a frame-indexed arcade oracle for actor, coordinate, colour, displayed value, highlighted cells, and pickup events. Force every row independently and compare natural and forced frame sequences; phase-label presence alone does not pass.

Acceptance criteria

  • All instruction actor identities, routes, placements, and phase durations are capture-backed.
  • Each pickup occurs only after a complete even red/yellow/blue cycle and at the required colour.
  • EXTRA, SPECIAL, and ×2/×3/×5 characters light at their corresponding pickups.
  • Both bottom point rows show the active multiplier after heart collection; collectible value changes correctly with colour.
  • Natural and forced captures match the oracle and preserve every prior actor underlay.
  • Module, cold-payload, combined, and future-sound limits remain passing.

Assignment contract

Approved objective; unassigned pending completion of the arcade oracle and a complete Ready contract. Expected artifacts are arcade capture data, src/presentation_runtime.s, focused presentation verifiers, generated evidence, this ticket, index, and log. No live-game scoring behavior may be changed.

Verification and completion

Pending capture-backed natural and forced frame sequences for every row, visual comparison, pre-emption checks, and capacity evidence.
