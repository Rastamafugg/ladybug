PERF-010 — Adaptive primary overlay call overhead Proposed

State: Proposed; not Approved, Ready, or an implementation result. Class: High-risk because this work touches a hard timing boundary, persistent framebuffer replay, and a nearly full resident region.

Objective and baseline

Reduce the measured three-enemy color-worklist cost by removing the per-pair subroutine call and return in the adaptive primary sparse-overlay path. This is a bounded performance increment. It cannot close BUG-058’s 27,000-cycle target by itself, and neither the projection nor this draft makes a Done claim.

The evidence uses source revision 163d0be13daeeea4f6ec2a1e6b027ef54e13447c, complete keyboard ROM SHA-256 7caa58c1ff1ef84b0b107b4b5cc46850244de1f2729dbc60f3af1f46abfac32e, and source-build receipt SHA-256 43b33e655a0c005eb6a51a3f0f2351186152721a9212b3f74702a0974191fc8c. See the cost summary, the corrected matched profile receipt, and its base profile.

Cycle values are sampled XRoar fast CPU cycles from event_ticks/8, scoped from input-parser entry through return. The named scenario is a controlled enemy-release worklist after natural credit and Enter entry. It is not a whole-game frame or whole-game maximum. Three samples with three enemies measured 68,575, 43,066, and 46,717 cycles; their maximum is 68,575, mean 52,786. The target is 27,000, so the sampled peak exceeds it by 41,575 cycles. Each row advanced two logical steps with no fault or remaining debt. Owner is secondary metadata; the profiled peak began with owner 0.

The same profile includes a separate controlled two-enemy sample of 87,609 cycles, without a call-cost profile. Keep it distinct from the three-enemy baseline and do not attribute it to this hot path. Eight quiet four-enemy samples peaked at 51,260 cycles; eight steady four-roaming samples peaked at 44,538 cycles. Both four-enemy sequences followed intentional audio silence. The two natural demo setup rows cost 1,144,507 and 1,148,051 cycles in their startup scopes and are not interactive timing rows.

Confirmed cost and mode accounting

In the profiled 68,575-cycle row, adaptive_render cost 55,878 cycles inclusive (3,138 exclusive of instrumented children). frame_render_background cost 44,874 inclusive over three calls (454 exclusive); compose_enemy_zone cost 21,571 inclusive; and draw_enemy_stage cost 13,472 over four calls. Parent and child spans overlap, so do not add them.

The positive sparse-overlay mode used OBJ_ACCENT=1. It entered replay_entity_overlay_common six times, once for each of six entities, with entity_count=8 and entity_work 6 through 1. Those calls were all primary-paint mode and cost 21,727 inclusive/exclusive cycles in total. render_entity_colour cost 22,849 inclusive, of which the instrumented overlay accounts for 21,727. actor_closure_restore cost 1,240; actor_closure_draw cost 3,499 inclusive; there was no capture entry in this peak.

The negative sparse-overlay mode is cache rebinding through rebind_cache_value; its per-pair cost was not captured in this peak. Zero mode is full cached-pair replay and was also not captured in this peak. The inline candidate is limited to positive primary paint. Rebinding and full replay retain their current code and behavior. A separate 20-cycle sync_entity_cache_colour span is not a measurement of the negative per-pair rebind loop.

audio_adaptive_api cost 4,457 inclusive over four calls; nested spans included aas_elapsed 935, aas_admit 1,674, aas_output 1,793, aas_prime 703, audio_mix 824, and audio_mix_write 580 cycles. These spans overlap and are CPU-cost measurements only. The backend was null, so there is no audible-output comparison.

Bounded source change

At src/main.s, inline the exact four-instruction primary-mask body only when ADAPTIVE_RENDERING=1: sta OBJ_VALUE, anda #$33, orb a,y, and tfr b,a. Keep the existing bsr primary_cache_mask in the nonadaptive branch. Wrap the existing primary_cache_mask/cck_primary definition and body in ifeq ADAPTIVE_RENDERING, emitting the helper only for AD0. This keeps primary-mask formation, flags at the following compare, cache value, registers, pixels, sparse records, geometry, journal ownership, and publication behavior unchanged. No other executable references were found in src/ or scripts/; cck_primary is a source-reference alias.

The isolated assembly shows inline code adds6 bytes at the sole callsite and removing the unused9-byte helper yields a net3-byte resident reduction for AD1. Keep the change bounded to src/main.s plus the routine description in scripts/source_reference.json, clarifying that the helper is emitted for AD0 while AD1 uses the inline equivalent. Current source-reference verification has no routine-symbol presence assertion; do not add a generic conditional-verifier framework. Add only focused map checks for helper present in AD0 and absent in AD1, then run existing source-reference generation/coverage in the complete profiles.

Expected improvement and hard ceiling

The source bounds a normal object to 29–37 cached pairs. Six primary-overlay calls therefore cover an estimated 174–222 pairs. On a 6809, replacing a two-byte BSR plus RTS with the same inline body removes 12 nominal cycles per pair: BSR costs 7 and RTS costs 5 in Motorola’s MC6809 programming manual, Appendix F. Pair counts and total savings remain projections, not measured candidate results. The projected saving is 2,088–2,664 cycles. The projected three-enemy worklist remains 65,911–66,487 cycles, or 38,911–39,487 cycles above target.

Even the impossible-to-assume ceiling of removing all 21,727 measured sparse-overlay cycles would leave 46,848 cycles, still 19,848 above target. The proposal can advance one measurable stage; additional causes or a separate ticket remain necessary to meet 27,000.
