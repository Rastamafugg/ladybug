Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Add complete CoCo presentations for EXTRA and SPECIAL completion so each authored intermission path, reward, tune and return handoff is visible in the 1.0 game. Reuse the existing game reward and audio-event owners where possible. Do not create a general animation/controller framework.

Evidence and baseline

  • The CoCo EXTRA map and the CoCo SPECIAL map contain the authored event screens. FEAT-010's right-HUD changes to these shared maps are intentional inputs. The EXTRA Ladybug Path now ends at the shared connector start; it contains no per-life destination path.
  • The CoCo template is a FEAT-010 HUD cleanup target: it removes CREDIT content and defines the reorganized right HUD, including future second-player score placement. It is not an EXTRA connector-generator output; FEAT-011 reads it as a reference and preserves FEAT-010 HUD edits in shared maps.
  • The presentation runtime owns screen flow and presentation handoffs. Its fixed range is $1900-$1DFF (1,280 bytes), with the stack floor at $1E00. Do not relax this boundary or assume spare bytes.
  • The game runtime owns life markers and game-state work; the shared text runtime maps reserve slots 0–11 to authored HUD positions.
  • The existing audio poller maps the SPECIAL event to cue 11 and the EXTRA event to cue 10. Reuse those event identities; do not add duplicate cue IDs or enqueue a second copy of the same completion cue.
  • The event-art compiler collects used tile IDs and emits a deduplicated atlas plus per-map tile lookup tables. The emitted bundle does not currently contain the maps' per-cell screen layouts or Ladybug Path polylines. The runtime contract must carry those authored records without duplicating route coordinates in assembly.

The previous CoCo SPECIAL implementation awarded 10,000 points; the approved 1.0 target is 100,000 points. The roadmap now records both the implemented baseline and the pending 1.0 target. The user confirms the retained EXTRA comparison: completing EXTRA awards one reserve life and no separate completion points. No additional MAME comparison or score-RAM decoder research is in scope.

Scope

In: the SPECIAL and EXTRA intermission entry/exit flow; adapted movement paths from the two CoCo maps; their existing event cues; the specified rewards; reserve-life counts above 12, saturating at 255, with a display limited to 12 markers; non-skippable input behavior; source, map, build/capacity and manual acceptance; and two named non-release E-pickup fixtures for focused repeat checks. The fixtures do not replace natural acceptance on the final release image.

Out: two-player gameplay (the second-player HUD position remains future layout preparation), dinosaur mini-games (post-1.0), changes to unrelated scoring or ranking, new sound cues, broad audio-priority changes, automatic performance benchmarking, test-only guest-state writes outside the two named fixtures, an interactive letter-toggle/cheat menu (a possible 1.1 feature), new generic path/controller or start-state frameworks, TOOL-015 qualification, controller-service adoption, emulator diagnosis, ROM creation outside the accepted build gates, and unrelated FEAT-010 work.

Adopted user-visible behavior

  1. Input: neither intermission can be skipped. Inputs during the animation cannot shorten or restart it. The completion handoff must not inherit an unintended held or buffered input.
  2. Timing and music: move at the adopted arcade pace and play exactly one complete celebration cue. EXTRA uses cue 10; SPECIAL uses cue 11. The next screen is not entered until the movement and full cue have completed. This is functional pacing, not an automated performance-acceptance target.
  3. SPECIAL: travel from the Lady Bug sprite's center to the coin's center, moving from right to left and then upward. The final sprite position must fully overlap the coin so pickup is visible. During the intermission, draw the Part Vegetable at the destination derived from SPECIAL's Sprite Locations marker. Award 100,000 points, then continue through the existing completion/return flow.
  4. EXTRA: loop around the middle garden, then turn left before descending past the target row; travel right below the maze into the reserve-life display and turn into the new life tile. During the intermission, draw the Part Vegetable at the destination derived from EXTRA's Sprite Locations marker. Award one reserve life and no completion points on every completed EXTRA while the count is below 255. At 255, continue the route, cue, and animation but retain 255 instead of wrapping to zero. Keep the display at no more than 12 markers; when all markers are occupied, the extra-life animation overlaps the 12th marker. A numerical counter above 12 is outside this 1.0 scope.
  5. Life count and display: reserve lives continue to count above 12 and saturate at the one-byte maximum 255; the HUD displays at most 12 reserve markers. The active life remains separate. This 1.0 scope adds no numerical counter for values above 12.
