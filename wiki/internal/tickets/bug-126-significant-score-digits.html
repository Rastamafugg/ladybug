Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Match arcade player-score formatting: show one 0 at zero, then add tens, hundreds, thousands, and higher digits only after each threshold instead of padding the six-digit field with zeros.

Evidence and baseline

User reports six-place zero padding and contrasts it with the arcade. Source path and ROM identity are unknown. Done BUG-061 concerns PART formatting, not score formatting.

Scope

In: active player-score display in live play and demo. Out: PART, TOP/ranking widths, score ownership, and scoring rules.

Scenarios and edge cases

Reported behavior is a six-place zero-filled player score. Expected behavior is significant digits only. Source and arcade geometry are not independently checked.

Options and recommendation

Reuse current score values and drawing routines where source review supports it. Do not change fixed-width ranking fields.

Acceptance criteria

At zero show one zero; at 9 one digit; at 10 two; at 100 three, continuing at decimal thresholds. Preserve score values in live play and demo.
