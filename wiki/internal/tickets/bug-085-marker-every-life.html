BUG-085 — Marker departure on every replacement life Proposed

High-risk · P2 · created 2026-10-01 · source fe160ae389145d24083218ded05ba9cdca781474

Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Run the HUD marker departure and maze-entry sequence for every replacement life, not only the initial live start.

Reproduction and observed versus expected

Start a live game, then force one death with a reserve available. Current initial live entry has a separate marker transfer; the death replacement appears through the ordinary init_player path and enters without repeating that marker departure. The expected result is one complete approved BUG-084 departure for every replacement life.

Confirmed source cause and baseline

live_begin arms INITIAL_ENTRY_STATE for the initial entry. At dt_finish_blank, the death path decrements LIVES, clears old player validity, calls init_player, and clears death state; it does not arm the entry state. This source path confirms the lifecycle handoff is missing. BUG-039 provides historical evidence for the current replacement and owner-local player cleanup, not proof of this requested repeat behavior. No current-snapshot runtime result was captured; the uncombined fe160ae source build stops at active GID 633.

Scope, scenarios, and acceptance

In: route each surviving post-death life through the approved BUG-084 marker-out, hidden cleanup, and maze-entry states exactly once. Out: death animation, total-life rules, player speed, collectible rules, enemy replacement policy, and unrelated stage choreography.

Acceptance covers initial entry, one forced death with a reserve, natural follow-on play, and the final-life boundary. Each consumed reserve decrements the HUD once and triggers one departure; the final-life death at zero reserves reaches game-over without underflow or another transfer. Every surviving replacement leaves no stale marker/player pixels on either owner and cannot receive input until maze entry completes. Stage advance preserves life count and does not arm a second entry sequence.

Class, dependencies, and risks

High-risk: death-to-entry cross-phase handoff, life-counter authority, and persistent framebuffer history on both owners. Depends on BUG-084 so the departure trajectory and cleanup contract are fixed first. A missing forced death or final-life boundary is not passing evidence.

Decision requested

Approve repeating the approved marker departure for each surviving replacement life, dependent on BUG-084. Proposed; not Approved or Ready.

2026-10-01 — Replacement-life accounting and assignment

BUG-062 owns the occupied-slot grid; BUG-084 owns the approved rightward departure and clipping behavior. Initial entry consumes one marker in iet_wait_stage. A replacement consumes one marker only in dt_finish_blank, which decrements LIVES before respawn. For a surviving replacement, the post-decrement value is the index of the previously last-occupied BUG-062 slot; arm the BUG-084 departure from that root without re-entering the initial wait/decrement path. Do not decrement again. If no reserve remains, preserve the current game-over branch without a departure. Stage advance does not arm entry. This preserves the selected total life budget and avoids duplicate consumption.
