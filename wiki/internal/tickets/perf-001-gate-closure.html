PERF-001 — Gate Closure Proposed

Reduce gate diagonal/final frames to the engineering target without weakening gate fidelity.

Identity and ordering

  • Status: Proposed; explicit user approval required
  • Created / updated: 2026-07-31 / 2026-07-31
  • Priority / order: P1 / 1
  • Owner: unassigned
  • Dependencies: none; shares bank-3/runtime artifacts with PERF-002 and PERF-003, so implementation is ordered
  • Source: post-acceptance performance work

Approval brief

Objective and baseline

Reduce complete current-map gate owner A/B intervals from 32,835/36,837 active cycles to at most 27,000. These are measured complete-frame maxima from the integrated Task 6 revision, not projections. Required savings are 5,835/9,837 cycles.

Work and recommendation

Replace or avoid the measured 7,694-cycle intersecting-static-object redraw and reduce B-owner replay/actor closure. Prefer a bounded generated or cached representation that reuses existing decoders and preserves the current overlap mask.

Scope

In: gate composition, overlapping static entity publication, replay and actor closure directly caused by the gate event. Out: gate rules, graphics, unrelated enemy AI, and a new framebuffer protocol.

Required scenarios

Diagonal, final, replay, overlap, no-overlap, both owners, dynamic dots, adjacent visual neighbor, and all 168 generated gate cases.

Acceptance

Every required complete interval is at most 27,000 cycles; pixel/entity output is exact; replay coalescing, bounded selection, PAR5 restoration, diagnostics, and capacity guards pass; current-map evidence records the source revision.

Assignment contract

Not ready. After approval, specify exact design, owned runtime/generator files, worktree, capture and verifier commands, retained traces/report, separate commit, integration order, and capacity reserve. Scope or target changes require renewed approval.

Decision log

  • 2026-07-31 — Proposed from integrated acceptance and gate attribution; not approved for implementation.
