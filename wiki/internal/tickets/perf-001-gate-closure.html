Approval brief

Objective and baseline

At gameplay-source revision 82e1524, two sequential but different gate worklists were measured: the diagonal/current interval was 32,835 active cycles while rendering into framebuffer A, and the following final plus pending-damage-projection interval was 36,837 while rendering into framebuffer B. A/B identifies the BACK target only; these values are not an owner-speed comparison. Reduce each required gate worklist to at most 27,000 cycles. Required savings from the captured intervals are 5,835 and 9,837 cycles.

Work and recommendation

Replace or avoid the measured 7,694-cycle intersecting-static-object redraw and reduce pending projection and actor closure in the final/replay worklist. Prefer a bounded generated or cached representation that reuses existing decoders and preserves the current overlap mask.

Scope

In: gate composition, overlapping static entity publication, replay and actor closure directly caused by the gate event. Out: gate rules, graphics, unrelated enemy AI, and a new framebuffer protocol.

Required scenarios

Diagonal/current, final with pending projection, natural replay, overlap, no-overlap, both framebuffer targets, reversed starting owner, dynamic dots, adjacent visual neighbor, and all 168 generated gate cases.

Acceptance

Every required scenario-phase/worklist interval is at most 27,000 cycles regardless of framebuffer target; pixel/entity output is exact; replay coalescing, bounded selection, PAR5 restoration, diagnostics, and capacity guards pass; current-map evidence records the source revision and owner as secondary metadata.

Selected design

Use current-address attribution to remove the generic intersecting-static-object redraw from the critical gate worklist through a bounded generated, cached, or specialized representation that reuses the existing destination-delta/PAR5 contract. Preserve the four-bit roaming-overlap mask. After the static redraw is removed, optimize only measured pending-projection or actor-closure work needed to close the final worklist. A new framebuffer protocol, unbounded gate cache, weakened entity fidelity, or owner-specific shortcut is prohibited. Competing in-scope representations may be benchmarked; rejected experiments must be reverted and recorded.
