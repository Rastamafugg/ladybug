Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Reconstruct the FEAT-002 failure and recovery sequence, determine which observable diagnostic and requirements-handling decisions delayed the repair, and convert accepted findings into precise updates for the project role skills. The intended outcome is a repeatable process that prioritizes software delivery and execution-state evidence, translates user-visible launch behavior into explicit state transitions, bounds runtime probes, and requires natural end-to-end verification before acceptance.

Evidence and baseline

  • Requested behavior: the conversation begins with an unattended title, instructions, part-one level-start, and demo loop; 5/6 add credit; and 1 starts normal gameplay after the level-start screen. On 2026-08-09 the user clarified that the authored attract TMX must be the first visible cartridge screen and that this replaces gameplay-first launch.
  • Natural failure evidence: the user reported a mostly black attract sequence with incorrect or non-animated actors and no successful 5-then-1 start. Later manual execution confirmed that the repaired cartridge loads and accepts credit/start, but new Draft blockers BUG-008 through BUG-013 record remaining natural presentation failures in the assigned worktree.
  • Probe escalation history: PERF-004 records 120- and 60-second loader observations, a 1427.1-second phased run without post-handoff markers, a 720-second breakpoint attempt inside startup, temporary defaults of 300/1800/60/180/180 seconds for loader/resident/natural/forced/demo, and a 315.1-second control observation. The 1800-second resident deadline was an extrapolation and was explicitly rejected as a hardware or acceptance target.
  • Premature hardware hypothesis: a register snapshot stopped at symbol address $C033, expected to be immediately before CLR $FF91, and directed the next investigation toward the Init1 or GIME path. No live-byte comparison had yet established that the bytes at $C033 were the resident instructions named by the current map.
  • Confirmed delivery defect: live-byte comparison subsequently showed bank-0 bootstrap bytes at the supposed resident handoff. Selected cartridge ROM prevented same-destination writes from populating resident RAM. The repair stages bank-1 resident and asset bytes in physical pages $21/$22, switches to all-RAM, then publishes them to pages $3E/$3F.
  • Confirmed bank-selection defect: boot synthesis switched to GMC bank 0 before reading authored bank-1 screen assets. Retaining bank 1 during synthesis restored byte-exact output.
  • Requirements-translation defect: gameplay initialization originally remained boot-owned. The later repair explicitly deferred gameplay construction until level or demo entry and made credit/start pre-emption global. This establishes that adding presentation calls was insufficient; the feature required replacing the existing startup owner.
  • Observation-tool defect: retained PERF-004 evidence rejects low-RAM trap snapshots because XRoar 1.10 snapshot capture at that boundary corrupts the serialized presentation module. Resident main_render boundaries replaced those captures.
  • Current reproducible baseline: main remains at 7135cbb. The assigned perf/perf-004-capacity-resolution worktree is based on the same revision and remains uncommitted; its tracked patch snapshot at proposal preparation has Git blob ID c9ac26b872bf9cc9d0a992506e47a17c27acba89. The built GMC SHA-256 is 9c16f657d0f22759da7b32452cf32eab39fc69f3d1bb819a51629177cdbe84f9.
  • Current verification observation: bash scripts/build.sh verify-gmc passed on 2026-08-09 in 37.8 wall-clock seconds. Its separate loader, resident, natural, forced-gameplay, and demo defaults are 10/10/10/20/10 seconds. This proves the current automated markers and structural worklists, not all user-visible FEAT-002 acceptance criteria.

Evidence classification and comparative finding

  • Root causes: resident/assets were not published to RAM while cartridge ROM was selected; boot synthesis read the wrong GMC bank; and presentation did not initially own replacement startup and global input pre-emption.
  • Contributing diagnostic failures: symbol intent was treated as live-code identity, probe deadlines expanded before marker semantics were validated, and hardware-path speculation gained weight before delivery and observation-tool defects were eliminated.
  • Contributing verification failure: structural and forced evidence was allowed to stand without proving the complete natural visual and input sequence. Current BUG-009 further shows that an attract-state marker does not prove that the attract TMX was the first published screen.
  • Luna/Sol comparison: using the user’s labels only, the Luna sequence emphasized progressively longer runtime probes, framebuffer-clear timing, and a possible GIME path. The Sol sequence first compared expected and live bytes, separated cartridge source from RAM destination, checked bank ownership, and used phase-specific bounded probes. The evidence supports the latter diagnostic order; it does not establish a hidden model-level cause.

Resolved research questions

  • The first decisive missed check was byte identity at the reported PC and handoff destination. A short authored/staged/live comparison would have invalidated the resident-symbol interpretation before register-level speculation.
  • The minimum useful phase order is cartridge delivery, all-RAM handoff, resident entry, resident startup completion, presentation publication, input transition, and gameplay. Each phase needs its own marker and deadline.
  • The undocumented-hardware hypothesis received excess weight because the register snapshot was interpreted through a stale execution-state assumption. Platform maturity was not used as a prior, and independent reproduction or authoritative evidence was not required before escalation.
  • The default-flow requirement failed where presentation was treated as a hook into gameplay boot rather than the new boot-state owner. The displaced gameplay initialization path and global input policy were not made explicit soon enough.
  • Forced marker coverage demonstrated code reachability but not first-frame identity, choreography, input timing, or full natural sequencing. Those are independent acceptance dimensions.
  • Shared invariants belong in AGENTS.md and the ticket workflow. Intake belongs in project-management, transition semantics in business-analysis, the evidence ladder in debugger, and natural-versus-forced closure rules in QA.

Scope

In scope: an evidence-indexed timeline; observed-versus-inferred classification; causal and contributing-factor analysis; rejected or premature hypotheses; comparison of the diagnostic sequences labeled Luna and Sol in the conversation; requirements-translation analysis; exact proposed and, after approval, implemented changes to .agents/skills/project-management/SKILL.md, business-analyst/SKILL.md, debugger/SKILL.md, and qa-reviewer/SKILL.md; consideration of corresponding AGENTS.md or workflow changes; and focused checks proving the instructions are complete and non-conflicting.

Out of scope: claims about private model reasoning, personality, or capability based only on model names; changing application-wide Codex behavior; fixing additional FEAT-002 or gameplay defects; rewriting project architecture; weakening hardware acceptance; and treating this retrospective as evidence that FEAT-002 itself is complete.

Scenarios and edge cases

  • A symbol or map names code at an address, but live bytes differ because loading, copying, banking, relocation, or handoff is incomplete.
  • A hardware-register access appears implicated, but the same failure could originate in software state, an emulator control path, debugger timing, or a corrupt observation method.
  • A long-running probe produces no marker. The process must distinguish slow execution from a missed breakpoint, stale process, wrong phase, incorrect address, or probe defect within bounded deadlines.
  • A feature exists in code or can be forced in isolation, but the natural boot sequence never reaches it.
  • A user describes a replacement default flow. Requirements analysis must identify which existing owner is displaced, not merely where the new behavior is inserted.
  • Credit or start input arrives in any attract substate, is held, alternates, or is simultaneous. The owning state machine and acceptance behavior must be explicit.
  • New bugs are reported after a basic manual pass. QA must classify each against the parent ticket criteria before recommending closure or a separate bug ticket.
  • Evidence from different revisions or dirty worktrees conflicts. The retrospective must preserve provenance and must not merge observations into one implied current state.

Options and recommendation

Option A, narrative only: record a prose postmortem without changing role contracts. This is low effort but does not prevent recurrence.

Option B, evidence-backed process correction: adopt the exact shared and role-specific changes below. This is recommended because it places each safeguard at the role that must execute it while retaining cross-role invariants in shared policy.

Option C, broad agent rewrite: redesign all project skills. This exceeds the observed problem and risks conflicting or duplicated instructions.

Exact proposed project-instruction changes

AGENTS.md — add shared runtime-diagnosis invariants under Rules for getting work done:

Before interpreting a symbol, map label, or hardware-register access at a live PC, prove that the live bytes match the current built artifact at that address. When loading, copying, banking, relocation, or handoff is involved, compare authored/source, staged, and destination bytes before diagnosing downstream execution.

Treat an undocumented hardware defect as a last-stage hypothesis. Do not recommend it until software state, loader/mapping, observation-tool, and emulator-control candidates have discriminating results, and either an independent emulator/physical-hardware reproduction or authoritative hardware evidence supports escalation. Label the hypothesis unconfirmed until then.

Every runtime probe must name its phase, success marker, deadline, and the meaning of a timeout. Keep an initial diagnostic phase at or below 60 seconds. Do not increase a phase beyond 60 seconds or by more than 2x without reporting measured progress, the projected benefit, and obtaining user approval. A timeout is evidence about the probe boundary, not proof that the target code is slow.

.agents/skills/debugger/SKILL.md — add a mandatory evidence ladder:

Before proposing a cause, execute this evidence ladder in order unless a step is demonstrably irrelevant:
1. Reproduce the exact artifact and record revision, dirty-state or patch identity, command, and observed versus expected result.
2. Bound the failure with phase markers. Each probe names one phase, marker, deadline, and timeout meaning.
3. At the first unexpected PC or data boundary, compare live bytes/data with the current built artifact. For loaders or banked code, compare source, staged, and final destination bytes.
4. Validate MMU/PAR/register state and the observation method. Check whether breakpoints, snapshots, traps, or control connections alter or corrupt the state being inspected.
5. Rank software, build/delivery, mapping, tool, emulator, and hardware hypotheses by evidence and prior likelihood; run the shortest test that separates the leading candidates.
6. Escalate an undocumented hardware-defect hypothesis only under the shared project invariant.

After one bounded timeout, inspect the last proven marker and probe mechanics before extending the deadline. Do not infer target-code slowness from debugger or emulator wall-clock time without a control measurement.

.agents/skills/qa-reviewer/SKILL.md — add to responsibilities:

For user-visible flows, verify the natural golden path from the delivered cold artifact before using forced-state evidence. Forced tests prove rare paths or reachability; they do not substitute for first-frame identity, sequence, timing, input pre-emption, or visible output.

Require expected screen/state identity and visible-output evidence, not only execution markers. A marker named attract does not prove that the attract asset was published.

Before recommending closure, confirm the tested artifact is revision-identified and integrated, classify every current user-reported defect against the parent acceptance criteria, and treat any missing required natural scenario as a failure.
