Replace the 92-box natural death-reset perimeter publication with a bounded exact projection.

Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Natural death-to-reset currently redraws 92 perimeter boxes before publishing the nest reset. The retained PERF-002 closed-interval evidence reports 241,720–242,969 active cycles for that combined worklist, far above the 27,000-cycle target. Symbol coverage identifies exactly 92 calls to draw_perimeter_box; this is worklist attribution, not a framebuffer-owner effect.

Evidence and baseline

At the final tested PR #3 head represented by build/perf002-death-reset.json, twelve measured closed intervals cover zero enemies, four enemies, and vegetable state across framebuffer targets A and B. The minimum is 241,720 cycles, the maximum is 242,969, and every observation fails by at least 214,720 cycles. Isolated structural-cache publication is 9,801–11,112 cycles, so PERF-003 common actor work cannot plausibly remove the perimeter-dominated excess.

Scope

In: native generated perimeter-reset payload, its bounded low-RAM publication helper, source loading/mapping, A/B ownership/replay, and verification. Out: changes to death cadence, gameplay rules, nest reset cache semantics, generic structural compositor behavior, gameplay-time ROM switching, and PERF-003 work.

Scenarios and edge cases

Force isolated perimeter publication and natural death-to-reset for zero, four, and vegetable structural states, starting with both framebuffer targets and reversed order. Prove 92-box-equivalent exact pixels, old-footprint/ownership behavior, PAR5 restoration, death cadence, and compatibility with the retained generic path. Missing forced cases fail verification.

Options and recommendation

Generate an exact native reset payload at build time, place source in the bank-0 overflow pool, copy it at boot to unused physical page candidate $3A, and invoke a small low-RAM helper at candidate $06B2-$07FF. Map PAR5 only for the bounded copy and restore it before return. Do not use $28-$2B, which are reserved for the compatibility compositor. Reject the current generic 92-box path and a likely-too-slow per-box direct-copy loop if measured evidence confirms they miss target. No gameplay-time ROM switching is permitted.

Constraints and risks

The helper has at most 334 candidate bytes after the bank-0 proof destination, physical page $3A must be proven unused, and interrupt-visible state must remain outside the temporary PAR5 mapping. The implementation must restore PAR5 to page $34 on every exit. Cartridge source headroom does not relax low-RAM, physical-page, code-window, direct-page, stack, ABI, or compatibility limits.

Acceptance criteria

Each complete natural combined perimeter-plus-cache-publish worklist is at most 27,000 active cycles, with target margin defined as 27,000 minus measured cycles. Every timed interval is closed from frame_render_impl to the next such boundary; tails are diagnostic only. Exact pixels, 92-box semantic coverage, A/B/reversed replay, PAR5 restoration, cadence, diagnostics, total cartridge capacity, destination capacity, clean build, and compatibility verifiers must pass.

  • Commands: clean detached scripts/build.sh build, dedicated capture/verifier, verify_nest_animation.py, verify_enemy_runtime.py, verify_performance_baseline.py, GMC/capacity verifier, and wiki lint/link checks using fail-fast execution.
  • Evidence and completion: retain current-source hashes, closed boundaries, symbol counts, target/margins, raw trace references, exact pixel proof, capacity and mapping state, rejected alternatives, one commit, and a report against every criterion. Parent owns integration and merge.
