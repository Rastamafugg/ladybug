Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Use a leading zero for single-digit PART values, keep tens/units fixed, and place hundreds immediately left for values above 99 wherever the game shows a part label/value.

Evidence, reproduction and cause

The complete gameplay HUD already calls asset_draw_two_digits, which writes a leading zero for values 1–9. The shared stage-panel formatters in src/shared_text_stage.inc and src/shared_text_ranking.inc suppress a zero tens digit and emit one to three significant digits. STAGE is documented as 1–255 while FEAT-005’s selectable starting level is 1–99. The omitted zero for 01–09 is source-confirmed. The user confirmed a hundreds digit immediately left of fixed tens/units for 100–255: “ 01”, “ 99”, “100”, “255”; 255→1 clears the hundreds cell.

Scope

In: static PART labels/field width and dynamic formatting on gameplay, level-start and any other screen that displays the current part. Out: changing stage progression, vegetable selection or clamping behavior without renewed approval.

Design and prototype

Gameplay currently starts its two-digit writer at x37, one cell left of the authored field. Move the writer so tens/units stay at x38/x39; reserve x37 blank for 1–99 and for hundreds at 100–255. Level-start side HUD uses the same fixed field. The central level-start PART label currently occupies x17–20, with a gap at x21 and value at x22. Move only that label to x15–18, keep a gap at x19, and reserve x20–22 for hundreds/tens/units; this preserves units at x22 and keeps hundreds from overwriting the T. The map edit is after approval.

Scenarios and edge cases

  • Values 1, 9, 10 and 99 display exactly 01, 09, 10 and 99 in every approved PART field; no stale third digit or one-cell shift remains.
  • All screen labels/value cells have consistent two-digit placement and colors; gameplay, next-level, level-start and other current-part displays agree.
  • Values 100/199/200/255 show their hundreds digit immediately left; 255→1 clears it while preserving the fixed two-digit cells.
  • Current complete build, shared-text static checks and natural first-part plus stage-transition display pass after layer and FEAT-005 dependencies.
