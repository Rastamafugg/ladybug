Approval brief

Objective and baseline

At gameplay-source revision 82e1524, the forced death-reset plus structural-nest worklist measured 35,590 active cycles when rendered into framebuffer A and 35,585 when rendered into framebuffer B. Buffer owner is secondary metadata; the five-cycle difference is not treated as an owner effect. Reduce every required death-reset worklist interval to at most 27,000 cycles. Required savings from the captured intervals are 8,590 and 8,585 cycles.

Work and recommendation

Replace the full 16-by-32 death-reset rebuild with a bounded generated transition or native cache path. Retain the complete compositor for release, vegetable, and genuine mixed-overlap changes.

Scope

In: death-reset structural nest publication and its persistent replay. Out: death presentation timing/graphics, release behavior, vegetable rules, and unrelated movement.

Required scenarios

Forced death-reset worklists into both framebuffer targets from identical starting state, reversed starting owner, natural A/B replay, reset from active enemies, reset with vegetable state, structural fallback, and unchanged death cadence.

Acceptance

Every required scenario-phase/worklist interval is at most 27,000 cycles regardless of framebuffer target; output matches the full compositor pixel-for-pixel; death cadence, ownership, PAR5 restoration, diagnostics, and combined capacity pass.

Selected design

Add a distinct death-reset nest intent and publish a prebuilt exact 16-by-32 reset image instead of invoking the generic structural compositor. The primary storage candidate is the free 256-byte game-state interval $A590-$A68F; build the cache when the authoritative nest background is captured, combining the clean background with dormant frame zero. If post-PERF-001 capacity or measurement rejects native caching, an existing-decoder destination-delta representation is the permitted fallback. Old roaming enemy footprints must still be restored. Release, vegetable, skull, and genuine mixed-overlap changes retain compose_enemy_zone.

Activation baseline

Integrated main revision 29309a2dc78e81b686a7c70ace9dd9ea3eee8724 measures the forced death-reset plus structural-nest worklist at 35,597 active cycles targeting A and 35,592 targeting B. These are two controlled observations of the same worklist, not evidence of an owner-speed effect. Required reductions are 8,597 and 8,592 cycles. Capacity is resident 8,190/8,192 bytes, assets 7,144/7,680, bank 3 4,075/4,096, direct-page spare 95 bytes, stack spare 1,791 bytes, and CPU-readable cartridge source spare 14,731 bytes. Runtime destination limits remain independent.
