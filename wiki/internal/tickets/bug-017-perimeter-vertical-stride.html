BUG-017 — Perimeter timer uses compressed vertical geometry Proposed

Place timer boxes on the authored maze border and remove the duplicate death-reset border.

Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Render all 92 release-timer boxes on the existing 8-pixel-grid maze perimeter. Enemy death must reset their colour without drawing a second compressed border or merging vertical blocks.

Reproduction and observed versus expected

  1. Run integrated ROM f412729c1ef09989666c360e81eba0c570556fe0c9e089cc0ecc059dec6c518d.
  2. Enter the part-one demo and wait for natural enemy collision at route index 66.
  3. Observe the duplicate interior bottom border and merged vertical timer blocks.

Observed: draw_screen advances TMX rows by 8 scanlines, while draw_perimeter_box, the generated reset payload, and its verifier multiply vertical cell coordinates by 5. Death queues RF2_PERIM_RESET and publishes all malformed positions at once. Expected: incremental and reset paths use the maze renderer's 8-pixel cell geometry.

Golden path

  1. Stage publication draws the authored border.
  2. Each timer expiry recolours one corresponding border box.
  3. The 92nd advance releases one enemy through the existing service.
  4. Death resets those same 92 pixels.
  5. Death art and all other maze pixels remain unchanged.

Scope

In: shared live/demo perimeter arithmetic, reset payload, independent oracle, both framebuffer owners, visible evidence, capacity, and timing. Out: release cadence/count, AI, maze TMX, death choreography, and framebuffer protocol.

Edge cases and risks

  • Corners and clockwise wrap must not duplicate or omit boxes.
  • Bottom-row output must stay inside 192 scanlines.
  • Incremental and reset paths must be pixel-identical.
  • The current verifier self-validates the defective formula; natural physical-frame evidence is mandatory.
  • The corrected span may change payload size or cycles.

Recommendation

Derive destinations from the canonical 8-pixel tile height. Do not compress the maze renderer or suppress reset publication.

Acceptance criteria

  • All 92 timer boxes coincide with authored border cells.
  • Natural enemy and forced skull death reset those locations without duplicate borders or merged blocks.
  • Part-one release remains 92 advances at nine committed frames per advance.
  • Both owners converge with zero FRONT writes and pixel-exact incremental/reset equivalence.
  • Payload size, reset cycles, builds, BUG-012 natural loop, and GMC verification pass.

Discriminating test

Compare every destination with tile_row * 8 * 160 + tile_column * 4 and a natural physical-frame crop. Mutating the multiplier to 5 must fail at the first non-top-row box.

Assignment contract

Not prepared. Ready preparation must define owned artifacts, commands, capture evidence, limits, worktree, commit boundary, and completion report.

Verification plan

Static mutation rejection; natural incremental circuit; enemy and skull resets; both owners; full before/after framebuffer; release timing; cycle trace; capacity build; BUG-012 loop; GMC verification.
