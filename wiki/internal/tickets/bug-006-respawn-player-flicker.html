Republish the replacement Ladybug into every persistent framebuffer owner before steady state.

Approval brief

Objective and motivation

After a life is lost, the replacement Ladybug must remain continuously visible while A/B framebuffer ownership alternates. The current symptom is intermittent disappearance that can continue until another death or collectible redraw.

Evidence and baseline

dt_finish_blank clears FBM_PLAYER_VALID for both owners, calls init_player, and sets RF_LIVES|RF_PLAYER for one render transaction. framebuffer_prepare_back hydrates the selected owner's validity, but actor_closure_draw draws the player only when RF_PLAYER|RF_STAGE or PLAYER_ERASED is set; it does not draw solely because the selected owner is invalid. A fresh XRoar respawn probe showed alternating front-owner/player-rectangle hashes after the forced reset, consistent with owner-local history not being republished.

Scope

In: death-to-respawn player validity, both framebuffer owners, alternating ownership, and interaction with pickup/death redraws. Out: death animation art/timing, lives rules, player movement, and enemy save-under protocol.

Scenarios and edge cases

  • Respawn from both initial front-owner states.
  • Zero and multiple active enemies, with and without pending background damage.
  • Natural alternating A/B publication for several frames after respawn.
  • Immediate collectible pickup and immediate second death.
  • Respawn at the same or a different player framebuffer address.

Options and recommendation

Recommended: make actor closure treat an invalid selected-owner player ledger as a required player draw while the game is alive, or explicitly carry respawn publication until both owner ledgers are valid. Preserve the existing buffer-local save-under semantics and avoid copying stale player bytes between owners.

Acceptance criteria

  • Both owner ledgers become valid for the replacement player before the first steady-state alternating sequence.
  • Player pixels remain present and correct across at least eight natural owner alternations after respawn.
  • Immediate pickup, death, popup, enemy overlap, and player movement remain correct.
  • Existing framebuffer ownership, BUG-001 through BUG-005, arcade-fidelity, GMC, build, and capacity checks pass.

Type-specific diagnosis

Observed: the replacement player flickers until another event redraws it. Expected: both owners contain the replacement before ordinary frames resume. Competing causes: stale player validity, stale save-under bytes, or scanout timing. Discriminating cause: after respawn invalidates both ledgers, the steady-state actor path has no condition that draws an alive player when the selected owner is invalid and no render flag is pending.
