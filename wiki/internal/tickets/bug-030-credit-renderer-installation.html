Recommended repair and capacity design

In the complete-profile common credit branch, call the existing install_highscore_runtime after adding the credit and before loading the high-score map selector. This replaces the incidental instruction/demo/audio owner at $0300 with the required high-score owner. Placing the installer before loading the selector avoids its documented accumulator clobber. Install once on the credit edge, not again on every hydrated screen load.

Static placement check: the existing installer at $19AE is within short-call range of the credit branch near $193B, so the added call is projected to cost two bytes. Recover those bytes by shortening two existing LBSR cold_ptr calls, currently at $1B7B and $1B98, to BSR in the complete profile. Their current short-branch displacements are +40 and +11 bytes; final assembly must confirm them after layout changes. Each saves one byte. This is a design projection, not an assembled candidate or an approved edit. Other profiles retain their emitted instructions through conditional assembly.

No new helper, relocation, memory region, ABI, or mapping protocol is needed. If this bounded placement fails, stop and revise the proposal rather than expanding a hard boundary or moving routines into another module.
