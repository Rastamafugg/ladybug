Cause and repair boundary

Complete-profile draw_recolored_map_tile reuses HUD_BYTE as glyph scratch after the first lit letter, corrupting the remaining scan. Repair the redraw's mask lifetime so each next bit comes from preserved word state or independent scratch, without changing pickup or reward logic. Clearing and reconstructing masks is rejected because it would alter reward state. High-risk class reflects stage-handoff publication, persistent framebuffers and resident capacity of 8191/8192 bytes; fitting may require equivalent size-neutral code.

Scope, acceptance and verification

In scope: word-progress redraw and only code/verification needed to preserve its mask across nested tile drawing. Out of scope: score amounts, life awards, pickup rules, game-over presentation and unrelated HUD colours. Acceptance: partial SPECIAL/EXTRA bits survive stage clear; exact selected letter pixels appear on both persistent owners after Part 2 gameplay publication with unselected letters unchanged; new-game reset remains intact; completing SPECIAL still awards its existing score and stage behavior; completing EXTRA still awards one life and its existing stage behavior; reward masks clear once as before. Verify controlled early/middle/late masks, natural credited Part 1-to-2 handoff, both completed-word rewards and new-game reset, then complete build, timing and capacity guards. Each scenario covers a distinct mask, owner, handoff or reward risk. If one-byte resident headroom prevents repair, present an equivalent fitting design for review instead of weakening behavior.
