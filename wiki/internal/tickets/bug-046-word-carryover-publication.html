Objective, report and baseline

Retain every partially lit SPECIAL and EXTRA letter through a level transition, until its word reward completes or the game session ends, as specified by the game design. The user reports missing lit letters after stage clear. Baseline is source b8f418f and complete 6809/keyboard ROM SHA-256 9bd7daa97e8ad93f08b31e0258afb20d21593bf6189e34926ee7fcc0ddaea1a9. The source-level defect below is confirmed by call and scratch-ownership inspection; a natural current-ROM stage-clear capture is still required to attribute the exact missing letters.

Discriminating source cause

src/main.s clears SPECIAL_BITS and EXTRA_BITS only at new-game initialization and completed-word reward, not in next_stage. The stage-background path in src/enemy_runtime.s calls draw_word_progress_hud after draw_screen. In the complete profile, that routine stores its shifting source mask in HUD_BYTE and calls draw_recolored_map_tile for each lit letter. The shared text path asset_draw_recolored_map_tile → asset_draw_hud_digit → shared_mask writes HUD_BYTE as glyph scratch, then returns to the caller's next lsr HUD_BYTE. The remaining mask is therefore no longer the saved SPECIAL/EXTRA bits. This explains missing or incorrect later lit-letter redraws without assuming state loss. A controlled mask-to-pixels test must confirm which patterns fail on the built ROM.

Diagnosis and acceptance boundary

Read-only next step: run a controlled stage-background publication with masks containing an early, middle and late lit letter, compare the stored mask with exact pixels on both persistent owners, then use a natural credited Part 1-to-2 clear with partial SPECIAL and EXTRA progress. The runtime probe begins from current authored/staged/live identity; phase marker is RF_STAGE publication, success marker is the first committed Part 2 gameplay image, initial deadline at most 60 seconds, and timeout means that marker was not observed within the probe.
