Root Cause & Arcade Reference

  • 1981 Arcade Z80 ROM ($07B5–$07EB): Compares pixel coordinates of Lady Bug ($6027/$6028) against each active enemy (HL+1/HL+2) and registers a hit if and only if |player_X − enemy_X| < 9 (≤ 8 px) and |player_Y − enemy_Y| < 9 (≤ 8 px).
  • Current CoCo 3 (src/enemy_runtime.s:1800–1819): enemy_player_contact compares only coarse 8-pixel cell indices (|PLAYER_CELL_X − enemy_x| ≤ 1 and |PLAYER_CELL_Y − enemy_y| ≤ 1), ignoring sub-cell step offsets (PLAYER_STEP, enemy 3,x, and movement directions). This allows contact at up to 14 px separation when actors move apart or diagonally near corners.
