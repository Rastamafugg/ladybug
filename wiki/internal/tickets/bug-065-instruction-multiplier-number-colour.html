Objective and motivation

Color the multiplier numerals 2, 3 and 5 blue wherever the instruction screen shows them beside the blue X glyph.

Evidence, reproduction and cause

The authored template X2/X3/X5 cells at (1–6,7) use raw character GIDs 147/465/147/449/147/417; these GIDs identify glyph shapes and do not encode blue. The current compiled template maps all six cells to GREY pen 7. Generated X2/X3/X5 replacements map X to BLUE pen 3 but their digits to WHITE pen 6 at destinations (27,17) and (27,20). The in-memory compiled-output candidate maps the six authored cells and generated digits to blue, changing 74 packed frame bytes without editing TMX.
