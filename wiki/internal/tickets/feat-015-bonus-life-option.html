    • Add BONUS LIFE to the Options menu (tiled/coco-options-screen.tmx, scripts/shared_text.py, src/shared_text_stage.inc) below LIVES: 0=DIFFICULTY, 1=LIVES, 2=BONUS LIFE, 3=STARTING LEVEL, 4=KEYBINDINGS, 5=BACK.

  2. Repeating Score Bonus-Life Award:
    • When MENU_BONUS_LIFE == 0 (OFF) or during unattended Demo mode (PRES_MODE == MODE_DEMO), no score-based bonus life is awarded (arcade EXTRA life only).
    • When MENU_BONUS_LIFE is 1..5 (10000..50000), crossing each repeating multiple of the configured threshold awards +1 reserve life (255-saturating, displaying up to 12 reserve HUD markers via RF_LIVES) and enqueues the extra-life audio cue (without an intermission).
    • Covers all live score additions (dots, bonuses, vegetables, and SPECIAL +100,000), including multi-threshold jumps (e.g. +100,000 on SPECIAL with a 10k–50k threshold awards the appropriate multiple of lives or advances the threshold cleanly without losing future awards).
  3. Memory, Docs & Verification Guards:

    • Update wiki/internal/game/overview.html and wiki/internal/implementation/roadmap.html to document that the 1981 arcade ROM has no score-based bonus life (EXTRA only) and CoCo provides this as an optional OFF-by-default setting.
    • Pass DOC-002, audio, presentation-flow, demo, and standalone 6809 bonus-life/options verification, followed by user XRoar RGB playtest.
