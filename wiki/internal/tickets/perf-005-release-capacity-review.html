Objective and motivation

After the game is functionally complete, review the full assembly and generated-data tree, restore every path temporarily bypassed by development profiles, and fit the complete release profile within native CoCo 3 and 64 K GMC cartridge constraints. Optimization must be driven by whole-tree evidence rather than repeated local byte squeezing during bug repair.

Scope

In: source/module/data inventory, call and reachability analysis, duplicate-code and duplicate-data removal, build-profile convergence, cartridge packing, copied-module and helper allocation, stack/direct-page review, cycle measurement, restoration of bypassed title/demo/high-score/name behavior, and release-capacity documentation. Out: changing arcade-visible behavior, dropping required game features, new gameplay rules, or accepting unsafe RAM overlap.

Scenarios and edge cases

  • Release and development profiles must share one functional implementation; conditional code may select features but may not retain divergent bug fixes.
  • Every restored presentation path must work in natural sequence and under credit/start pre-emption.
  • Rare paths, both framebuffer-owner orders, cold/warm entry, and source/staged/live byte identity remain mandatory.
  • A capacity pass with missing required behavior is failure. A behavior pass that exceeds a hard hardware boundary is also failure.

Evidence and baseline

The verified BUG-011 development artifact is one 2026-08-12 observation: cold data 9,704/10,874 bytes, presentation module 954/1,280, instruction helper 866/938, combined module/cold 10,658/14,219, 1,424 CPU-readable GMC bytes spare, future-sound margin -112/512, and a branch-complete BUG-011 worklist maximum of 21,645 cycles with 8,021 cycles of hardware margin and 5,355 cycles of engineering margin. The direct choreography draft had ended at $1FD4, 468 bytes beyond the $1E00 stack floor. These development values are inputs, not the future whole-tree baseline; PERF-005 must recapture both profiles and all cycle maxima from its assigned checkpoint.

Acceptance criteria

  • All temporarily bypassed behavior is restored and all functional ticket acceptance suites pass under the release profile.
  • The final GMC image fits 65,536 bytes and every copied/runtime region remains inside its documented hard boundary with no stack, loader, MMU, or direct-page collision.
  • The complete natural presentation/game sequence plus forced rare paths passes on the current artifact.
  • Measured scheduled worklists remain below the hardware cycle ceiling; any engineering-margin target is fixed during proposal from the current functional baseline.
  • The review records each accepted optimization, rejected experiment, bytes/cycles saved, behavior risk, and retained evidence.

Dependencies, risks, and ordering

First freeze behavior and tests, then inventory capacity, rank changes by whole-tree benefit and risk, apply one bounded optimization at a time, and rerun integration. Primary risks are accidental behavior loss, profile divergence, stale generated artifacts, relocation errors, and optimizing an incomplete path.
