Approval brief & root cause

Observed symptom

While watching attract mode cycle in the extra-e ROM (53cd2d40...), two horizontal flickering lines briefly corrupted the flower point value (10 POINTS) on the Instructions screen for one cycle; the next cycle was clean.

Confirmed source-traced cause

  1. On Instructions (tiled/coco-instructions-screen.tmx), 10 POINTS begins at tile (17, 17) = framebuffer address $7544. In extra-e, init_entities places the yellow E at cell (10, 18), whose 16×16 sprite root is also $7544, flanked top and bottom by horizontal maze corridor walls in PLAYER_BG_B.
  2. When Demo reaches (10, 18) in extra-e (or clears a stage on primary ROM), STAGE_PENDING becomes nonzero while Lady Bug is still alive (PLAYER_BG_VALID = 1, PLAYER_FB = $7544). demo_tick jumps immediately to demo_death_done → High Score without clearing PLAYER_BG_VALID. The same leak occurs on the primary ROM when a wake key returns to Attract during live Demo or mid-choreography Instructions.
  3. In src/presentation_runtime.s, load_done_hold_plain called PRES_MAIN_FB_CAPTURE on both Buffer A and Buffer B before load_done_normal cleared PLAYER_BG_VALID, re-recording FBM_PLAYER_VALID = 1 and stale FBM_PLAYER_FB into both FB_META_A and FB_META_B across High Score, Attract (PRESENTATION_ATTRACT_OVERLAY), and Instructions hydration.
  4. On Frame 1 of Instructions (Buffer A), irt_init cleared PLAYER_BG_VALID for Buffer A only. On Frame 2 (Buffer B), PRES_MAIN_FB_PREPARE hydrated PLAYER_BG_VALID = 1 and PLAYER_OLD_FB = $7544, causing restore_actor to stamp 16×16 stale corridor walls onto Buffer B at $7544 (30 Hz flicker over 10 POINTS). On cycles where Demo died to an enemy instead, dt_blank cleared FBM_PLAYER_VALID = 0 on both buffers, leaving the next Instructions screen clean.

Approved scope & fix

  • In scope: Add clr PLAYER_BG_VALID (2 bytes) immediately before jsr PRES_MAIN_FB_CAPTURE at load_done_hold_plain in src/presentation_runtime.s so every held presentation screen (High Score, Instructions, Game Over, Enter High Score, Options, Credits, Keybind Options) captures FBM_PLAYER_VALID = 0 into both FB_META_A and FB_META_B.
  • Out of scope: Changes to src/main.s, src/instruction_runtime.s, src/enemy_runtime.s, src/audio_runtime.s, or attract actor animation.
