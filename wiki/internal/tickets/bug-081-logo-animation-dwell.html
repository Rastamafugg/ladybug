BUG-081 — Arcade logo animation dwell Proposed

High-risk · P2 · created 2026-10-01 · baseline fe160ae389145d24083218ded05ba9cdca781474 plus nine unchanged dirty TMX inputs

Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Match each two-frame LadyBug logo hold to 31 CoCo Vbord ticks on both the attract and high-score screens. The attract dwell is measured at 31 complete MAME video frames. The high-score target of 31 frames is an explicit user assumption that it shares the attract timing; no high-score arcade dwell measurement is claimed or required. Preserve phase order, static logo colour, and the red R.

Observed behavior and arcade baseline

The user reports that both CoCo logo animations dwell for less time than actors. BUG-047 owns attract colour and BUG-052 owns high-score composition and animation; neither authorizes changing their cadence from an unmeasured high-score target. On current MAME 0.220, the exact attract-logo ROI has two pixel states and nine complete 31-frame holds, followed by ten observed state transitions including partial entry/exit. This is one attract result, not a high-score target. Counts and raw identities are in RSCH-014-B evidence.

Cause confidence

The current MAME 0.220 attract capture contains 17 complete 31-frame logo holds. The current CoCo source uses an eight-call logo phase cadence after its initial alignment in both contexts; the high-score cadence is also observed in BUG-052's current-ROM receipt. The frame-clock basis is now source-supported: the CoCo Vbord IRQ advances the frame counter, the presentation dispatcher consumes one new frame sample, and its attract/high-score timer writers advance once per presentation tick. Existing arcade-to-CoCo calibration maps one captured MAME frame to one 60-Hz CoCo Vbord tick. Thus the proposed implementation target is exactly 31 Vbords between published logo phases. Full source path and evidence boundaries are recorded in RSCH-014 B4 evidence.

Scope and acceptance

In: independent 31-Vbord logo phase holds on attract and high-score, with logo-only publication. Keep attract decorative actors at their existing eight-Vbord cadence. Out: logo art, palette, red R, high-score table/prompt/coin content, actor cadence, movement speed, and a scheduler-wide slowdown. Acceptance requires repeated 31-tick holds in both CoCo contexts, first and alternate owner pixel equality, unchanged non-logo cells and actors, and a complete build/capacity pass. The high-score arcade target remains a user assumption, not a measured result.

Class, risks, and lifecycle

High-risk: two presentation contexts use distinct destinations, and animation is published through persistent framebuffer owners. The attract helper also owns actor animation, so the logo's longer dwell must not slow decorative actors. BUG-047 static colour and BUG-052 high-score composition/animation are already Done; this proposal changes only their dwell target. Existing hard build windows and framebuffer publication remain acceptance risks.

Decision

Proposed. The user authorized the 31-frame high-score assumption and removal of the reachability-measurement gate. This does not approve implementation. Explicit approval is required before the repair can advance to Approved or Ready.
