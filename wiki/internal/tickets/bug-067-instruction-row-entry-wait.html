Approval brief

Objective and motivation

Remove the redundant full colour-cycle wait before first Ladybug movement in rows 2 and 3 while preserving row 1 and each earliest legal trigger-colour edge.

Evidence, reproduction and cause

The current generator applies row offsets (0,90,180), explicit earliest gates reference_consumes[4]+90 and reference_consumes[11]+90, and a target-colour transition boundary. These are integer ticks on the generated instruction timeline. Removing only the two explicit gates changes no event motion or consume tick and does not remove the wait. Current generated row-first motions are E=129, S=673 and heart_x2=1409; each affected row has a 143-tick first-motion gap from the preceding row’s final consume (530→673 and 1266→1409). Removing row offsets and both +90 floors together gives candidate first motions E=129, S=577 and heart_x2=1217; both later-row gaps become 47 ticks, a measured 96-tick earlier start relative to each preceding row consume after colour-edge alignment. Candidate row-start consumes at 600/1240 are 70 ticks after the preceding final consumes. Next-screen schedule changes from 1896 to 1884 (12 ticks shorter). These values are generated schedule ticks, not raw MAME frames; raw-frame equality is not claimed. Current and candidate event records are retained in bug067-row-entry-contract.json.

Scope

In: diagnose and remove one specifically identified redundant full colour-cycle wait before the first Ladybug movement at each affected row start. Out: changing actor speed/cadence, consume ordering, trigger-colour rules or broader instruction timing without an explicit measured target. No production edit has started; implementation remains gated on explicit approval.

Design and prototype

Remove row_time_offsets=(0,90,180) and both explicit +90 earliest gates together. Removing only the gates is output-identical dead-floor deletion and does not meet the request. The combined candidate removes the later-row cycle, keeps row 1 at 129 and shortens the schedule by 12 ticks.

Scenarios and edge cases

  • Record a current-versus-candidate timeline for all 16 events, including each row’s first motion tick, prior colour-loop boundary and all motion/consume tick deltas.
  • The candidate removes the 90-tick row offset at row 2 and row 3 while preserving earliest legal target-colour transition and consume order; alignment makes each relative first-motion gap 96 generated ticks shorter (143→47), and next-screen shortening is 12 ticks.
  • Natural instruction playback confirms the first movement after each affected row starts without the extra cycle. Prove current ROM/code identity, name a row-start phase marker and success marker, set a deadline no longer than 60 seconds, and report a timeout as inconclusive.
  • After the timing target is resolved and approved, run existing instruction/layer checks and the complete-profile build; retain the generated event table and natural-frame evidence.
