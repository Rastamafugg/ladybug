Objective and motivation

Remove the redundant full colour-cycle wait before first Ladybug movement in rows 2 and 3 while preserving row 1 and each earliest legal trigger-colour edge.

Evidence, reproduction and cause

The current generator applies row offsets (0,90,180), explicit earliest gates reference_consumes[4]+90 and reference_consumes[11]+90, and a target-colour transition boundary. These are integer ticks on the generated instruction timeline. Removing only the two explicit gates changes no event motion or consume tick and does not remove the wait. Current generated row-first motions are E=129, S=673 and heart_x2=1409; each affected row has a 143-tick first-motion gap from the preceding row’s final consume (530→673 and 1266→1409). Candidate row-start consumes at 600/1240 are 70 ticks after the preceding final consumes. Next-screen schedule changes from 1896 to 1884 (12 ticks shorter). These values are generated schedule ticks, not raw MAME frames; raw-frame equality is not claimed. Current and candidate event records are retained in bug067-row-entry-contract.json.

Scope

Out: changing actor speed/cadence, consume ordering, trigger-colour rules or broader instruction timing without an explicit measured target. No production edit has started; implementation remains gated on explicit approval.

Remove row_time_offsets=(0,90,180) and both explicit +90 earliest gates together. Removing only the gates is output-identical dead-floor deletion and does not meet the request. The combined candidate removes the later-row cycle, keeps row 1 at 129 and shortens the schedule by 12 ticks.
