CoCo 3 · hardware

Sound

Four audio sources gated through a PIA selector switch — 6-bit DAC, cassette, cartridge SND, hard-wired silence — plus a 1-bit square-wave source on PIA2 PB1.

Selector switch

The CoCo's audio mixer accepts four sources, gated by a "selector switch" implemented on PIA1's CA2 and CB2 plus a master enable on PIA2 CB2:

Selector A posSource
06-bit DAC on PIA2 PA7-PA2
1Cassette playback
2Cartridge SND pin 35
3Hard-wired low (silence)

Plus an independent 1-bit square-wave source on PIA2 PB1 that's always summed into the path when the master is on.

6-bit DAC (selector position 0)

  • PIA2 DRA $FF20 bits 7-2 form the 6-bit value (PA7 = MSB).
  • Output range 0 .. 4.5 V in approximately 0.07 V steps (PA7 = 2.25 V, PA6 = 1.125, PA5 = 0.563, PA4 = 0.281, PA3 = 0.140, PA2 = 0.070).
  • Setup: configure PIA1 CRA so CA2 outputs low and PIA1 CRB so CB2 outputs low (= position 0); then set PIA2 CB2 high (master on); ensure PIA2 DDRA bits 7-2 = 1 (output) and CRA bit 2 = 1 (DR access).
  • To play a tone, write a sine-wave-like sequence of values to $FF20 at a regular cadence. Pitch = inverse of write period; timbre = waveform shape.

The DAC also drives the cassette write path (via the attenuator on pin 4 of the CASS jack).

1-bit square wave on PB1

  • PIA2 PB1 (bit 1 of $FF22). Configure DDRB bit 1 = 1 (output), then toggle PB1.
  • Limited timbre but very cheap — no DAC table to feed; just an EORA #$02 / STA $FF22 pair plus a delay loop. The CoCo 1/2 manual's SLTON listing demonstrates exactly this for a sliding-pitch tone.
  • Sums with whatever the selector switch routes.

Selector setup gotcha

The selector switch state is split across two PIAs (PIA1's CA2/CB2 select the position; PIA2's CB2 is the master). Both PIAs need their CR bits configured for "CA2/CB2 = output, hold value = bit 3 of the CR".

Selector A (audio) and selector B (joystick comparator input) are the two halves of one switch and therefore share PIA1 CA2/CB2. Selecting right-joystick X or Y on B simultaneously changes A from cartridge SND position 2 to DAC position 0 or cassette position 1. PIA2 CB2 is a master gate for A only; it cannot preserve cartridge audio while B selects a joystick axis. The documented internal circuit provides no independent cartridge-audio bypass or sample-and-hold path.

PB1 remains independently summed, but it is only a one-bit source and cannot reproduce the GMC's three tone channels plus noise. Software using analog joystick axes and GMC audio must therefore accept brief source interruptions, schedule samples around audio, reduce sampling frequency, or change the control/audio hardware contract.

Sources: Tepolt, Assembly Language Programming for the Color Computer ch. 10 (DAC, selector, comparator, listings 10-2 SLTON); Tepolt, Assembly Language Programming for the CoCo3 ch. 7 ("split FF22": bits 2-0 still drive PB1/CB2/etc.).
