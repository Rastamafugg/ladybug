6809 · Arithmetic

SBCA — Subtract with Carry from A

A ← A − (M) − C

SBCA  <operand>

Description

Subtracts (M) + C from A. Used after SUBA to propagate the borrow through higher bytes of a multi-precision subtraction.

Condition codes

EFHINZVC
