6809 · Logical

ANDA — Logical AND with A

A ← A AND (M)

ANDA  <operand>

Description

Bitwise AND of A with the operand. Use to mask bits (ANDA #$0F keeps the low nibble).

Condition codes

EFHINZVC
––––↕↕0–

Addressing modes

ModeSyntaxOpcode~#

Examples

        ANDA   #$0F        * keep low nibble
        ANDA   <mask
