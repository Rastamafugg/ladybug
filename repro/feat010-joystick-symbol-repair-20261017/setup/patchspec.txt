1. In the existing presentation-module map exporter, add identity entries to its current wanted map and required-symbol set for exactly PRESENTATION_MAP_INSTRUCTIONS, PRESENTATION_MAP_HIGH_SCORE, PRESENTATION_MAP_LEVEL_START, and MODE_LEVEL. The guarded equates therefore must be active for keybinding_runtime.s only.

Source authority and control values: baseline complete presentation map at repro/feat010-frontend-dispatch-repair-20261016/candidate/build/ladybug-presentation-runtime.map.
