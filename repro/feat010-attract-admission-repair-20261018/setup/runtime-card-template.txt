A1-R — one changed-ROM natural no-input branch/retry diagnostic

Distinct risk: a queued presentation publication defers the actor cache write and prepare call, and the still-current phase can be admitted after publication clears.
Prerequisites: passing A1-S, one passing A1-B, one passing A1-P, and parent-frozen source/staged/live instruction spans for the new ROM. Freeze the decoded addresses from the compiled listing/map; the projected values below are expectations, not byte identity proof.
Launch: reuse repro/feat010-attract-next-diagnosis-20261017/runtime-command.js.txt and its owned-process/cleanup mechanics. Use its pinned XRoar settings, the new candidate ROM, no input and the approved GDB-MCP listener. Verify the port is unused and process identity before launch. No user-window routing.
Projected stops, all unconditional one-shot breakpoints: (1) $078E, the BNE after `TST PENDING`; (2) $07E3, `pao_done`; (3) $0792, before the unchanged `JSR PRES_MAIN_FB_PREPARE`. Parent replaces these with the exact compiled values only after A1-P and records all three source/staged/live spans before any PC interpretation.
At every stop: compare authored, staged and live bytes at the exact PC and required preceding instruction span; print raw PC/DP/CC; require PC exact and DP=$00 before symbol interpretation; use only the approved fixed direct-page read `x/231xb 0x0002`. No MMIO, stack reads, guest writes, input, forced state, conditional breakpoints, extra stops or retries.
Stop 1: capture PRES_ACTOR_PHASE as P0. Require CC.Z=0, proving the immediately preceding `TST PENDING` observed nonzero. PENDING itself may have cleared after TST; interrupt return restores CC. Stop 2: require phase remains P0 and the qualified BNE led to the byte-matched `pao_done` RTS without the phase write or prepare call. Stop 3: require PENDING=0, phase equals the result recomputed from PRES_TIMER by the pinned `presentation_attract_phase`, and the JSR bytes target the map-qualified PRES_MAIN_FB_PREPARE. Do not hardcode old observed phase 3.
Retain direct-page state already allowed by the card: FRAMES, PRES_TIMER, phase, PENDING, ACTIVE, FRONT/BACK IDs, commit/missed/fault counters, mode/screen, menu select/dirty, and relevant logo cache fields. No additional read class is authorized.
The “RTS destination” proof is constrained to matched compiled BSR target/range/return continuation, the map/listing-qualified phase RTS path, and exact live bytes at `pao_done`. This template authorizes no live stack peek. If that does not satisfy the approved card interpretation, report the gap to parent before Ready; do not add a stack read.
Stop cap: three unconditional one-shot stops and at most three continues, one per stop; each continue timeout is at most five seconds. Whole phase maximum 45 seconds from owned launch through detach/session close and verified cleanup. Begin owned cleanup by second 37 and reserve the final eight seconds for cleanup. Prior old-ROM 7.797-second observation is historical; the changed-ROM run is unmeasured. Timeout is setup/observer failure or missing coverage at the last proven marker, never a product pass. First mismatch, command error, timeout, unexpected PC/DP or ownership mismatch ends the attempt.

Uncertain/incomplete observation, absent witness, or absence of visible symptoms without full-visit coverage is missing coverage. No pixel-capture or FRONT-readback gate. A2 is excluded.
