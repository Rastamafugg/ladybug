FEAT-010 Options observer failure review

Disposition: observer/setup failure; no product defect reproduced and no cause confirmed.

Observed result: The one cold run used ROM 274d09 and ended INCOMPLETE in 11.173 seconds, with 3 of 12 stop/continue observations and no GDB-MCP timeout. Full live resident bytes at C116 and presentation bytes at 1900 matched their pinned artifacts. During the conditional A=6 wait, GDB reported breakpoint-hit at 1A78 while the four-second host SIGINT guard was also dispatched. The script correctly rejected the overlap before its stop-sync, PC read, or selected-screen snapshots. Signal-versus-breakpoint ordering is unproven. No callback state, framebuffer diagnostics, or Options overlay/freeze witness was collected; Credits was not launched.

The user reports pressing Enter, seeing only the attract menu, and then seeing the window close. The record confirms the owned XRoar process was terminated by cleanup after the observer rejection; that closure does not establish a product crash. The user did not separately confirm the preceding Down press/release or the full input-edge history. No native response or Options failure was qualified.

Bounded correction proposal for a future single Options case: Remove the scheduled host SIGINT from every expected-breakpoint wait, including the native input/selection wait. Use the existing GDB-MCP continue call with a five-second timeout and at most five seconds of recovery, within the existing ten-second per-stop/continue limit. Advance only on a normal breakpoint-hit at the expected PC; a timeout, error, signal stop, or unexpected PC is observer-incomplete, retains the last proven evidence, and stops without retry. Keep the existing four-second owned-GDB SIGINT guard only for the final unpaused witness after the approved breakpoints are deleted; accept its capture only after signal-received and stop synchronization. An early or raced natural stop remains incomplete.

Keep the existing independent cold Options sequence and read set: native Down press/release, then Return press/release; artifact identity before interpreting live code; user visual witness of the reported overlay/freeze while unpaused. Preserve the 45-second whole-phase deadline from owned launch through cleanup, twelve-observation cap, no guest writes/injected input/private monitor, no pixel-acceptance claim, and no Credits scenario. If any required boundary or user witness is absent, report incomplete or not reproduced. The approved card says no retry; this proposal authorizes no run and requires renewed parent/user release before execution.
