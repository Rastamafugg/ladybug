Bounded direct-page size recovery

The three candidate-only operand overrides are limited to original-capsule listing sites $1B65, $1B94 and $1B9B (the later addresses shift after insertion): `ldb PRES_SCREEN` F6 00 A6 to `ldb <PRES_SCREEN` D6 A6; `tst PRES_HOLD_OWNER` 7D 00 D9 to `tst <PRES_HOLD_OWNER` 0D D9; and `sta PRES_HOLD_OWNER` B7 00 D9 to `sta <PRES_HOLD_OWNER` 97 D9.
