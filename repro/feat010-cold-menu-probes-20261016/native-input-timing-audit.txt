The proposed single 15-second selected-entry continue is within the existing GDB-MCP interface declaration, which accepts a numeric timeout with a 30-second default. The proposal still needs renewed user approval because it changes the local wait from 5 to 15 seconds, with up to 5 seconds of recovery. A notification immediately before this continue gives the user a clear input-start anchor. It does not prove that the menu is visible then. Fifteen seconds begins when continue_exec starts; the visible-menu interval may be shorter or absent. The record must include the user's report of actual key sequence and whether usable menu time occurred. If visibility or action is not established, classify setup-incomplete.

Budget audit: the proposed 37-second admission threshold is the sum 15 seconds input + 14 seconds allocated post-selection work + 8 seconds cleanup. The measured prior setup reached the wait with 39.257 seconds remaining, giving 2.257 seconds of margin against admission; this is one observation, not a guarantee. The 14-second post-selection allocation can fit after a selected breakpoint returns naturally at or before 15 seconds, because no timeout recovery is then needed. It cannot simultaneously guarantee 14 seconds after a full 15-second wait plus 5-second recovery: that path needs 42 seconds including cleanup. At the 37-second admission threshold, a full 15+5 timeout leaves only 9 seconds before the 8-second cleanup reserve, so timeout snapshots may be partial. The proposal must say that recovery consumes the shared post budget on the no-marker branch and that raw failure reads are best-effort; otherwise the 37-second threshold understates the claimed budget. If 14 seconds is meant to remain after recovery, admission must be 42 seconds, which the prior 5.743-second setup observation would not satisfy.

The 45-second whole-phase deadline remains feasible as a hard stop, not as a guarantee that all seven planned observations will complete. Selection may consume 15 seconds; the three callbacks and final witness remain bounded by the shared remainder. Existing waits stay at 5 seconds plus at most 5 seconds recovery. Guards must stop before a call when its remaining interval and 8-second cleanup reserve do not fit. Missing callbacks, the final witness, or full timeout evidence then means incomplete diagnosis; do not extend the deadline or retry. The observer cap of 12 remains above the seven planned continues and does not grant additional time.
