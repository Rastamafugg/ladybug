FEAT-010 R2 Options captured-state audit

Result: options-r2-result.json records a 17.212 s phase within the 45 s limit, four continues, three expected breakpoint hits, and a normal final owned-GDB SIGINT stop. There were no GDB tool errors or timeouts. User separately confirmed the Options/collectible flicker persisted until the window closed. The user did not separately confirm input unresponsiveness; this is not a pixel-readback acceptance result.

Byte identities are qualified against the pinned capsule (ladybug.rom SHA-256 274d09efd147373bc3d931ed3ab71354d316b93614a3abbb641e642eecb8fa53). The full resident dump was accepted as the bank-1 binding. At final PC C117, captured bytes are 0d 91 26 fb bd 0a 00 10; those bytes exactly match authored ladybug-runtime.rom offset 0x117 and packaged ladybug.rom bank-1 offset 0x4117. Only after that match, map symbol mainloop=C116 places C117 at the main loop's pending check. This is a single interrupted PC sample, not proof of a permanent wait.

All 1271 bytes at 1900..1df6 matched the pinned presentation runtime at the initial, selected, and final captures. Selected PC 1A78 bytes (91 a6 27 08 0f e0 0f e1) therefore match the pinned module, whose map labels 1A78 as start_screen. The selected stop is at routine entry with A=6 (Options); PRES_MODE/PRES_SCREEN still read 02/00, the prior attract state, because start_screen has not yet stored its arguments. At final stop they read 05/06. PAR5 reads 23 at selection and 34 at the final stop.

The 938-byte helper dump at 0300 matched the authored instruction runtime exactly (SHA-256 32116bc664e91054a70fdb1d4763242820c281afd0ae0c2d52cffb9f8d6f0654) at both selected and final snapshots. It did not match the demo (143 bytes) or high-score (917 bytes) payload, including their prefixes. The immutable capsule source facts pin instruction bytes at page 35/A422, demo at A7CC, and high-score at A880, with authored and packaged hashes equal; the same payloads occur in the pinned ROM at offsets F495, F83F, and F8F3 respectively. The captured helper identity is thus the instruction payload, not a mismatched helper. The snapshot does not show when or which earlier call copied it. Source flow permits that payload for attract screen 0; screen 6's start_screen path does not request an instruction install.

At the selected stop, raw framebuffer bytes 8F..91 were 01 00 00; at final stop they were 00 01 01 (front, back, pending). The script read 8F..92, so it captured only the high byte at FB_COMMIT_SEQ=$0092 and omitted its low byte at $0093. No sequence value or zero/unchanged commit claim is supported. FB_MISSED_COMMIT raw bytes changed 00 22 to 00 23; record this as a diagnostic delta only. Final PC C117 with pending=1 and render-active=0, plus one missed-commit increment, is insufficient by itself to establish a freeze or cause. The manual visual witness is separate evidence of flicker, not proof of commit failure.

Remaining uncertainty: the captures establish the selected start_screen entry, current presentation-module identity, instruction-helper identity, and one final main-loop state. They do not establish whether the Options load reached load_done/load_done_publish, whether commits continued after the final sample, the exact rendered FRONT pixels, or why the user-visible flicker occurred. No cause is confirmed.

If a further probe is later approved, the smallest useful read-only trace is the natural Options path from start_screen(A=6) through load_done/load_done_publish, retaining current-PC progression and native-input response, with complete u16 FB_COMMIT_SEQ ($0092..$0093) plus the existing front/back/pending and missed-commit baseline/delta. Keep it a fresh cold launch with the existing 45 s/12 observation limits; no pixel-readback claim unless that oracle is separately qualified. This is a proposal only; no additional probe or capture was run.
