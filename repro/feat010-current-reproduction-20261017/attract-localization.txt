Ranked explanations and limits

A Vbord commit during that second copy could expose a partial or mixed owner image. This is a concrete unsafe path, not a confirmed visual cause. Separately, actor phase logic intentionally maps logical phase 3 to source phase 1, yielding a `0,1,2,1` cycle; repeated frames at that cadence are expected and must be separated from abnormal replay.
2. **A2 leading source-specific hypothesis:** a Down edge can update selection/dirty state after a logo publication has already become pending. That commit may publish the old selection before the selected BACK owner is repainted; dirty owners are repaired in separate transactions. This explains a transient stale highlight opportunity. The source does not prove that old pixels were actually on FRONT or that owner identity caused the user-visible rollback. A value-flow error or another visual path remains possible.
