Call and data trace

The retained patch adds a guarded call at the entry to draw_entities, before the existing entity-overlay loop. It runs only when RF_ENTITIES is set and RF_STAGE is clear. The new routine scans all 20 gate/entity records; when a record count is nonzero, it skips IDs matching RENDER_GATE_ID, RENDER_GATE2_ID, or GATE_ANIM_ID, then calls draw_gate.

  • Entity refresh at enemy_runtime.s:766 restores entity footprints, then calls draw_entities. The patch hook therefore runs after underlay restoration and before the current entity overlays.
  • Full stage setup at enemy_runtime.s:909 calls draw_entities after drawing all gates. The patch's RF_STAGE guard is intended to bypass its repair scan on this path.
  • Hidden gate rendering at main.s:3063 calls draw_entities after draw_gate and before draw_player. Whether the render flags suppress or enter the new hook here is unresolved.

draw_gate restores the gate background and overlays the selected gate and its listed neighbour. Exact ordering through its overlay, entity-cache, tile-draw, and actor callees was not proven in this bounded pass. The patch's ID skips do not establish safety for framebuffer-local pending intents or a settled neighbour next to a pending gate.

GATE_COPY_COUNT is direct-page byte $79 and is reused by the patch as the 0–19 loop counter. The candidate then calls draw_gate inside the loop for nonempty associations. Preservation across the complete compositor call chain and the framebuffer IRQ path is unproven. This review does not certify the counter as safe. The record count is stage-built and may include consumed entities; the live-entity filter and resulting final layer were not traced through every callee.

No-overlap cycle estimate

The candidate listing at C667 resolves the zero-association loop to these instructions: LDA direct 4, LDB immediate 2, MUL 11, ADDD immediate 4, TFR D,Y 6, LDA 4,Y 5 (indexed 8-bit offset form), taken BEQ 3, INC direct 6, LDA direct 4, CMPA immediate 2, and short BLO 3: 50 cycles per record. Twenty records cost 1,000 cycles; entry CLR direct adds 6 and final RTS adds 5, for 1,011 cycles in the repair routine.

For the qualifying path, the hook adds LDA RENDER_FLAGS 4, BITA 2, not-taken BEQ 3, BITA 2, not-taken BNE 3, and LBSR 9: 23 cycles. Total estimated static increment is 1,034 cycles per no-overlap call. This is derived from the retained candidate listing and standard 6809 instruction timings. It is not a complete-frame observation and does not establish a numeric publication-window margin. No source-matched complete-frame threshold is available; historical BUG-005/PERF values do not satisfy that requirement.

One correction direction: gate the compositor call on a proven overlap indication produced by the already-required footprint restoration, so a no-overlap worklist bypasses the 20-record scan. The static no-overlap work potentially avoided is about 1,020 cycles (1,011 routine plus the 9-cycle subroutine call). The bytes and cycles needed to produce and preserve that indication are unknown; no cycle-neutral fast path is demonstrated. Treat this as a design direction for a later bounded assignment, not a validated correction.
