Competing explanations

- A2: changed selection may be repainted late or an older owner may be restored after the new selection. It may be coupled to A1, but no shared cause is established.
- A visual report alone can confirm presence or absence in the observed interval; it cannot establish the executed write or actual FRONT bytes. Keep A1 and A2 separate unless one observed sequence discriminates them.
